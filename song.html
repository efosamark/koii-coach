K.O. II / Coach

Chapter 2 · About 60–90 minutes

Make it a song

Turn your beat into a 16-bar song with an intro, a melody and a breakdown.

The melody is four notes, placed on the grid like your beat.

You will make: a 16-bar song, about 43 seconds, from four scenes in a song list.

Before you start

  • Your Chapter 1 beat, in project 6
  • BEAT’s number on your paper
  • OS 2.0 or newer

What you need

Your beat from Chapter 1 · First beat, drums and bass, in project 6.

Paper and a pencil, with BEAT’s scene number. You add INTRO, MELODY and BREAK.

OS 2.0 or newer, for the song list in Part 5.

Your pad plan

Part 1 · Make the intro

Goal: copy BEAT as INTRO and take out its bass, then copy BEAT again as MELODY.

  1. Go home, and stop playback

    Press MAIN. If the K.O. II is playing, press PLAY to stop.

    Check: Nothing plays.

    Why

    BEAT stays untouched: you build each new section in a copy of it.

    Official guide: Modes

  2. Select BEAT

    Hold MAIN and use − / + until the display shows BEAT’s number from your paper. Release MAIN.

    Check: The display shows BEAT’s scene number while you hold MAIN.

    Not working?

    No number written down? Hold MAIN, step through scenes with − / + and play each briefly. Do not press ERASE now. See help: “Which scene am I in?”.

    Official guide: Modes

  3. Check that BEAT plays

    Press PLAY, listen, then press PLAY to stop.

    Check: You hear drums and bass.

    Not working?

    Skipped the bass in Chapter 1? Do its Part 4, “Add a bass”, first.

    Official guide: Modes

  4. Make a copy for the intro

    With BEAT selected, press SHIFT + MAIN once. Hold MAIN, read the new number and write it down as INTRO.

    Check: The new number differs from BEAT’s.

    Why

    Commit (SHIFT + MAIN) saves this scene and moves you into a new copy of it. Changes here leave BEAT as it is.

    Not working?

    Commit is not a backup: it makes a new scene inside this project.

    Official guide: Modes

  5. INTRO: select group B

    In INTRO, tap B once, without holding it, to select group B. Press MAIN and check playback is stopped.

    Check: Nothing plays, with group B selected.

    Official guide: Modes

  6. Make the intro: remove the bass

    Hold MAIN first: the number must be INTRO, not BEAT.

    Removes only the B1 notes on these two steps.

    With − / + go to 1.1.1 and briefly press ERASE + B1, then the same at 1.3.1.

    Erased the wrong note? Press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

    Check: At 1.1.1 and 1.3.1 pad B1 no longer lights.

    Why

    An intro is smaller than the main beat. A brief ERASE + pad removes only the note on the current step.

    Not working?

    Group B jumped to another pattern? You held B while pressing − / +: see help “− and + change the wrong thing”. Never use ERASE + SOUND: it deletes the sample file. Erased the wrong thing? See help “I erased or changed the wrong thing”.

    Official guide: Play and record

  7. Check the intro and your original

    Press PLAY, listen to four loops and press PLAY to stop. Then hold MAIN, select BEAT, press PLAY, then stop.

    Check: INTRO: kick, snare and hats, no bass. BEAT: drums and bass.

    Hear the beat
    Why

    Copies keep their own notes: changing INTRO did not change BEAT. That is what makes commit safe. See Learn: “Groups and scenes”.

    Not working?

    BEAT lost its bass? You erased in BEAT: press SHIFT + B at once while the umbrella is lit, or write B1 again at 1.1.1 and 1.3.1 with RECORD + B1. INTRO still has bass? Repeat the two steps before this one, in INTRO.

    Official guide: Modes

  8. Make a copy for the melody

    With BEAT still selected, press SHIFT + MAIN once. Hold MAIN, read the new number and write it down as MELODY.

    Check: Press PLAY in MELODY: drums and bass. Stop.

    Why

    The melody section starts from BEAT, so it keeps the drums and the bass. INTRO is finished as it is.

    Not working?

    No bass? You copied INTRO by mistake: hold MAIN, select BEAT, press SHIFT + MAIN again and write the new number as MELODY.

    Official guide: Modes

Checkpoint: INTRO plays drums only, while BEAT and MELODY play drums and bass.

If it doesn’t: BEAT lost its bass? You erased in BEAT: press SHIFT + B at once while the umbrella is lit, or write B1 again at 1.1.1 and 1.3.1. See help “Which scene am I in?”.

Part 2 · Set up the melody

Goal: in MELODY, put a quiet melodic sound on C1 and make group C’s pattern two bars long.

  1. MELODY: select C1

    In MELODY, tap C to select group C. Press SOUND and tap pad C1.

    Check: C1 plays its current sound, if it holds one.

    Official guide: Modes

  2. Choose a melody sound

    Hold SOUND, type 5, 0, 0 on the pads and release SOUND. Tap C1 to hear it.

    Check: C1 plays a melodic sound.

    Why

    Group C holds the melody. Factory melodic sounds are stored at 500–599.

    Not working?

    500 silent, or not to your taste? Browse 500–599 with − / + and pick a short sound you like. See help: “A pad is silent”.

    Official guide: Modes

  3. Make the melody quieter

    Stay in SOUND with C1 selected. Turn X down until the melody is clearly quieter than the kick and snare.

    Check: The melody sits behind the drums.

    Why

    A melody supports the beat. Too loud, and it covers the drums.

    Not working?

    Turn X only in SOUND. In MAIN, X sets the input level instead.

    Official guide: Modes

  4. Give the melody two bars

    Press MAIN and check that playback is stopped, with group C selected. Hold RECORD, press + once, then release both.

    Check: The display shows Ln.2: C’s pattern is two bars long.

    Why

    A and B keep repeating their one bar under C’s two. Extending adds empty space: it does not copy notes.

    Not working?

    Check that C is selected first: each group has its own length. Pressed + twice? Hold RECORD and press − once.

    Official guide: Play and record

Checkpoint: C1 plays a melodic sound behind the drums, and C’s pattern is two bars long.

If it doesn’t: each group has its own length: tap C first, then repeat Give the melody two bars.

Part 3 · Place the melody

Goal: place four melody notes across two bars, then tune them to fit the bass.

  1. Place melody note 1

    With playback stopped, in MAIN with group C, hold SHIFT and press − until the display shows 1.1.1. Hold RECORD, tap C1 once, then release RECORD.

    Check: C1 lights at 1.1.1.

    Why

    The first melody note starts bar 1.

    Not working?

    Without RECORD held, tapping C1 only plays it. Nothing is written.

    Official guide: Play and record

  2. Place melody note 2

    Use − / + until the display shows 1.2.3. Hold RECORD, tap C1 once, then release RECORD.

    Check: C1 lights at 1.2.3.

    Why

    The second note lands late in beat 2. That is all for bar 1.

    Not working?

    On the wrong step? Briefly press ERASE + C1 at that exact position.

    Official guide: Play and record

  3. Place melody note 3

    Use − / + until the display shows 2.1.1 (SHIFT + + jumps a whole bar). Hold RECORD, tap C1 once, then release RECORD.

    Check: C1 lights at 2.1.1.

    Why

    This starts the second bar with the same melody sound.

    Not working?

    The display must start with 2, for bar 2. Only ever 1.x.x? C is still one bar: repeat “Give the melody two bars”.

    Official guide: Play and record

  4. Place melody note 4

    Use − / + until the display shows 2.3.1. Hold RECORD, tap C1 once, then release RECORD.

    Check: C1 lights at 2.3.1.

    Why

    This finishes a simple four-note shape across two bars.

    Not working?

    Do not add more notes yet. Listen to these four first.

    Official guide: Play and record

  5. Listen to the melody with the beat

    Press PLAY. Listen to two full two-bar passes. Press PLAY again to stop.

    Check: You hear drums, bass and a four-note melody that repeats every two bars.

    Hear the beat
    Why

    This is the section your song comes back to.

    Not working?

    No bass? You are in a copy of INTRO: hold MAIN and compare the number with MELODY. Melody too busy? Briefly erase the note at 2.3.1 and listen again.

    Official guide: Play and record

  6. Fit the melody pitch

    Press SOUND and tap C1. Turn Y a small amount while tapping C1, and play the beat to compare.

    Check: The melody sounds right with the kick and bass.

    Keep the setting that sounds best with the bass.

    Why

    In SOUND, Y is pitch. A small change can make the melody fit the bass.

    Not working?

    Turn Y a little at a time. Nothing fits? Choose a different melody sample.

    Official guide: Modes

Checkpoint: drums, bass and a four-note melody that repeats every two bars.

If it doesn’t: only 1.x.x positions? C is still one bar: repeat Give the melody two bars. No bass? You are in a copy of INTRO: hold MAIN and compare the number with MELODY.

Part 4 · Make the breakdown

Goal: copy MELODY as BREAK and clear its kick and bass, so the song can drop and come back.

  1. Make a copy for the breakdown

    Stop playback and press MAIN. Press SHIFT + MAIN once. Hold MAIN, read the new number and write it down as BREAK.

    Check: Holding MAIN shows a new number. Press PLAY: it sounds like MELODY. Stop.

    Why

    MELODY is finished. The breakdown starts as an exact copy of it.

    Not working?

    From now on, check the scene number before erasing anything: MELODY must stay as it is.

    Official guide: Modes

  2. Make the breakdown: clear kick and bass

    Hold MAIN first: the number must be BREAK.

    Clears every note of A1 and B1 in this pattern.

    With playback stopped, press MAIN and tap A. Hold ERASE + A1 until TRK blinks, then release. Tap B and repeat with B1.

    Wrong notes gone? Press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

    Check: Press PLAY: melody, snare and hats, but no kick and no bass. Stop.

    Hear the beat
    Why

    A breakdown takes the heavy parts away, so they hit harder when they return. Holding ERASE + a pad until TRK blinks clears all its notes in this pattern at once.

    Not working?

    A brief ERASE press clears one step only. Hold until TRK blinks to clear the whole track. See help: “I erased or changed the wrong thing”.

    Official guide: Play and record

  3. Check all four scenes

    Hold MAIN and select INTRO, BEAT, MELODY and BREAK in turn. In each, press PLAY briefly, then stop.

    Check: INTRO: drums only. BEAT: drums, bass. MELODY: drums, bass, melody. BREAK: melody, snare, hats.

    Why

    Song mode only plays the scenes you made. If a scene is wrong now, the song will be wrong too.

    Not working?

    Fix a wrong one in its own scene now. Your paper list of numbers is your map from here. See help: “Which scene am I in?”.

    Official guide: Modes

Checkpoint: INTRO: drums only. BEAT: drums and bass. MELODY: drums, bass and melody. BREAK: melody, snare and hats.

If it doesn’t: fix a wrong scene in that scene, checking its number before you erase. See help “I erased or changed the wrong thing”.

Part 5 · Build the song list

Goal: put your four scenes in order in a song list: 10 positions, 16 bars.

Your song map

SectionSceneSong positionsBarsWhat you hear
IntroINTRO1–22Drums only: kick, snare and hats
Main grooveBEAT3–42Your Chapter 1 · First beat: drums and bass
MelodyMELODY5–64Drums, bass and the two-bar C melody
BreakdownBREAK72Melody, snare and hats; kick and bass removed
Melody returnMELODY8–94Kick and bass come back under the melody
EndingBREAK102The melody without kick and bass, then the song stops

Total: 16 bars. At 90 BPM in 4/4 that is about 43 seconds.

To make a section longer, give it one more song position with the same scene.

  1. Plan the song: one position, one pass

    Read the song map above: 1–2 INTRO, 3–4 BEAT, 5–6 MELODY, 7 BREAK, 8–9 MELODY, 10 BREAK.

    Check: The plan adds up to 16 bars: about 43 seconds at 90 BPM.

    Why

    Each position plays its scene once, for as long as its longest pattern. INTRO and BEAT last one bar, MELODY and BREAK two, because C is two bars long.

    Not working?

    Scene numbers and position numbers are not the same. Keep your paper list of scene numbers beside you.

    Official guide: Workflow

  2. Open the song list

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Stop playback. Hold MAIN and press ENTER to open the song list.

    Check: The display shows the current song position.

    Why

    Song mode plays the positions in order by itself.

    Not working?

    Nothing opens? Song mode needs OS 2.0 or later: update first, as in Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II. The list already holds a song you care about? Leave it, and use an empty project. See help: “The song is too short or has extra positions”.

    Official guide: Modes

  3. Set position 1 to INTRO

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Hold SHIFT and press − / + until position 1 holds INTRO’s scene number.

    Check: Position 1 holds INTRO.

    You will confirm it by listening at the end.

    Why

    A new list starts with one position.

    Official guide: Modes

  4. Add position 2: INTRO

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Hold SHIFT and press A to add a position, then, still holding SHIFT, use − / + to choose its scene. Choose INTRO for position 2.

    Check: Position 2 holds INTRO, the same as position 1.

    Why

    SHIFT + A adds a position and SHIFT + − / + picks its scene. A new position may not copy the one before, so set each one.

    Not working?

    Add while you are on the last position.

    Official guide: Modes

  5. Add positions 3–6

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Do the same four more times: 3 BEAT, 4 BEAT, 5 MELODY, 6 MELODY.

    Check: Release SHIFT and use − / + to step through the positions: there are exactly six.

    Not working?

    Extra position? Select it and press SHIFT + C to cut it. Wrong scene? Select the position and hold SHIFT + − / +.

    Official guide: Modes

  6. Add positions 7–10

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Add four more positions the same way: 7 BREAK, 8 MELODY, 9 MELODY, 10 BREAK. That makes nine SHIFT + A presses in total.

    Check: Release SHIFT, step through with − / +: exactly 10 positions, matching the song map.

    Why

    Repeating MELODY gives each melody section four bars, and the final BREAK works as a soft ending.

    Not working?

    Lost count? Step back to position 1 with − and check each position against the song map. See help: “The song is too short or has extra positions”.

    Official guide: Modes

Checkpoint: stepping through the list with − / + shows exactly 10 positions, matching the song map.

If it doesn’t: an extra position? Select it and press SHIFT + C. A wrong scene? Select the position and hold SHIFT + − / +. See help “The song is too short or has extra positions”.

Part 6 · Play the whole song

Goal: play the song from the list, add a little reverb, and make one change of your own.

  1. Play the whole song

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Press SHIFT + PLAY to play the song from the start. Listen without touching anything.

    Check: INTRO, BEAT, MELODY, BREAK, MELODY, BREAK in that order: about 43 seconds, then it stops.

    Why

    This is the moment your separate loops become one song.

    Not working?

    Much shorter, or a section missing? A position is missing or holds the wrong scene. Much longer? A pattern is longer than planned. Keeps looping? System setting 420 turns song loop off. See help: “The song is too short or has extra positions”.

    Official guide: Modes

  2. Start the song again for the reverb

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    In the song list, press SHIFT + PLAY to start it again.

    Check: The song plays from INTRO.

    Official guide: Modes

  3. A: add a little reverb

    Tap A, press FX and choose reverb with − / +. Raise the fader slightly: it sets how much of A goes to the reverb.

    Check: You hear a little reverb on the drums.

    Why

    A little reverb puts drums and melody in one room. Each group’s fader sets how much of it goes to the one shared reverb.

    Not working?

    Sounds blurry? Lower the fader. Do not hold FX while pressing a group button: that solos it.

    Official guide: Effects

  4. C: a little reverb too

    Tap C and raise its fader a little too. Leave B’s reverb low.

    Check: The song sounds more finished, while the kick and bass stay clear.

    Not working?

    If the song ends, start it again.

    Official guide: Effects

  5. Your song is ready to develop

    In the song list, play the song once more with SHIFT + PLAY. Say the presses for each move, then tap it to check.

    Official guide: Modes

    • Commit: SHIFT + MAIN, which moves you into a copy.
    • Select a scene: hold MAIN + − / +.
    • Remove notes: brief ERASE + pad removes one. Hold until TRK blinks to clear the track.
    • Song list: MAIN + ENTER opens it. There, SHIFT + A adds a position, SHIFT + − / + picks its scene and SHIFT + PLAY plays from the start.

  6. Make one change from memory

    Change one thing in your song without looking at the steps.

    For example, step to the last position with + and add one more BREAK position (SHIFT + A, then SHIFT + − / +). That adds about 5 seconds.

    Not working?

    Back up the project with the EP sample tool on a computer. To record audio, the K.O. II can send its sound over USB to a computer or phone. See help: “How do I export or back up?”. Next: Chapter 3 · Sample, chop & resample, where you record your own sounds.

    Official guide: Workflow

Checkpoint: INTRO, BEAT, MELODY, BREAK, MELODY, BREAK in that order: about 43 seconds, then it stops.

If it doesn’t: much shorter, or a section missing? A position is missing or holds the wrong scene. It keeps looping? System setting 420 turns song loop off.
