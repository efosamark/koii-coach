K.O. II / COACH

CHAPTER 2 / ABOUT 60–90 MINUTES

Make it a song

Turn your beat into a song

You already made the foundation. This chapter adds melody, variation, a breakdown and an arrangement, one small action at a time.

You will make: a 16-bar song, about 43 seconds at 90 BPM, from four scenes in a 10-position song list.

What you need

Your beat from Chapter 1 · First beat, in project 6, with its drums and bass, and the scene number you wrote down for BEAT.

Paper and pencil: you name four scenes in this chapter, BEAT, INTRO, MELODY and BREAK, and write their numbers down.

OS 2.0 or newer for the song list (Part 5). This course is written for OS 2.5.1.

Part 1 · Make the intro

  1. Go home, and stop playback

    Press MAIN. If the K.O. II is playing, press PLAY to stop.

    Why

    Your finished beat stays untouched: you build each section in a copy. You will name four scenes in this chapter, BEAT, INTRO, MELODY and BREAK, so keep paper handy.

  2. Select BEAT

    Hold MAIN and use − / + until the display shows the scene number you wrote down for BEAT (drums + bass) in Chapter 1. Release MAIN.

    Not working?

    No number written down? Hold MAIN, step through scenes with − / + and play each briefly. Do not press ERASE now. See help: “Which scene am I in?”.

  3. Check that BEAT plays

    Press PLAY to check you hear drums and bass, then press PLAY to stop.

    Check: Playback is stopped and BEAT (drums + bass) is selected.

    Not working?

    Skipped the bass in Chapter 1? Do its Part 4, “Add a bass”, first.

    Official guide: Modes

  4. Make a copy for the intro

    With BEAT selected, press SHIFT + MAIN once. Hold MAIN and read the new number on the display; write it down as INTRO.

    Check: The INTRO number is different from BEAT.

    Why

    Commit saves the current scene and creates a new copy, and you are now in that copy. Changes here do not overwrite BEAT.

    Not working?

    Commit is not a backup file; it makes a new scene inside this project.

    Official guide: Modes

  5. INTRO: select group B

    In INTRO, tap B once (do not hold it) to select group B. Press MAIN and make sure playback is stopped.

  6. Make the intro: remove the bass

    Use − / + until the display shows 1.1.1 and briefly press ERASE + B1. Go to 1.3.1 and briefly press ERASE + B1 again.

    Erased the wrong note? Press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

    Check: At 1.1.1 and 1.3.1 pad B1 no longer lights.

    Why

    An intro is smaller than the main beat. A brief ERASE + pad removes only the note on the current step.

    Not working?

    Holding B while pressing − / + switches group B to another pattern instead of selecting it; see help “− and + change the wrong thing”. Never use ERASE + SOUND: it deletes the sample file. See help: “I erased or changed the wrong thing”.

    Official guide: Play and record

  7. Check the intro and your original

    Press PLAY, listen to four loops and press PLAY to stop. Then hold MAIN, select BEAT and press PLAY: its bass is still there. Stop.

    Check: INTRO: kick, snare and hats, no bass. BEAT: drums and bass.

    Why

    Copies are independent: changing INTRO did not change BEAT. That is what makes commit safe.

    Not working?

    If BEAT lost its bass, you erased in BEAT: press SHIFT + B at once (while the umbrella is lit), or write B1 again at 1.1.1 and 1.3.1 with RECORD + B1. If INTRO still has bass, repeat the two steps before this one in INTRO.

    Official guide: Modes

  8. Make a copy for the melody

    BEAT is still selected from the last step. Press SHIFT + MAIN once. Hold MAIN and read the new number; write it down as MELODY.

    Check: Press PLAY in MELODY: drums and bass. Stop.

    Why

    The melody section starts from BEAT, so it keeps the drums and the bass. INTRO is finished as it is.

    Not working?

    No bass? You copied INTRO by mistake: hold MAIN, select BEAT, press SHIFT + MAIN again and write the new number as MELODY.

    Official guide: Modes

Part 2 · Set up the melody

  1. MELODY: select C1

    In MELODY, tap C to select group C. Press SOUND and tap pad C1.

  2. Choose a melody sound

    Hold SOUND, type 5, 0, 0 on the pads and release SOUND. Tap C1 to hear it.

    Check: C1 plays a melodic sound.

    Why

    Group C holds the melody. Factory melodic sounds are stored at 500–599.

    Not working?

    If 500 is silent or unsuitable, browse 500–599 with − / + and choose one short sound you like. See help: “A pad is silent”.

    Official guide: Modes

  3. Make the melody quieter

    Stay in SOUND with C1 selected. Turn X down until the melody is clearly quieter than the kick and snare.

    Check: The melody sits behind the drums.

    Why

    A melody supports the beat. Too loud, and it covers the drums.

    Not working?

    Turn X only in SOUND mode; in MAIN, X is input gain.

    Official guide: Modes

  4. Give the melody two bars

    Press MAIN and make sure playback is stopped, with group C selected. Hold RECORD, press + once, then release both.

    Check: The display shows C’s pattern length change from 1 to 2.

    Why

    This makes C’s pattern two bars long. A and B stay one bar and simply repeat underneath. Extending adds empty space; it does not copy notes.

    Not working?

    Check that C is selected first: each group has its own length. Pressed + twice? Hold RECORD and press − once.

    Official guide: Play and record

Part 3 · Place the melody

  1. Place melody note 1

    In stopped MAIN with group C, hold SHIFT and press − until the display shows 1.1.1. Hold RECORD, tap C1 once, then release RECORD.

    Check: C1 lights at 1.1.1.

    Why

    The first melody note starts bar 1.

    Not working?

    Tapping C1 without holding RECORD only auditions it; nothing is written.

    Official guide: Play and record

  2. Place melody note 2

    Use − / + until the display shows 1.2.3. Hold RECORD, tap C1 once, then release RECORD.

    Check: C1 lights at 1.2.3.

    Why

    The second note lands late in beat 2. Two notes are enough to start.

    Not working?

    Placed it on the wrong step? Briefly press ERASE + C1 at that exact position.

    Official guide: Play and record

  3. Place melody note 3

    Use − / + until the display shows 2.1.1 (SHIFT + + jumps a whole bar). Hold RECORD, tap C1 once, then release RECORD.

    Check: C1 lights at 2.1.1.

    Why

    This starts the second bar with the same melody sound.

    Not working?

    The display must begin with 2: that means bar 2. If you only ever see 1.x.x, the C pattern is still one bar; repeat “Give the melody two bars”.

    Official guide: Play and record

  4. Place melody note 4

    Use − / + until the display shows 2.3.1. Hold RECORD, tap C1 once, then release RECORD.

    Check: C1 lights at 2.3.1.

    Why

    This finishes a simple four-note shape across two bars.

    Not working?

    Do not add more notes yet. Listen to these four first.

    Official guide: Play and record

  5. Listen to the melody with the beat

    Press PLAY. Listen to two full passes (each pass is two bars). Press PLAY again to stop.

    Check: You hear drums, bass and a four-note melody that repeats every two bars.

    Why

    This is your melody section: drums, bass and melody together.

    Not working?

    No bass? You are in a copy of INTRO: hold MAIN and compare the number with MELODY. Melody too busy? Briefly erase the note at 2.3.1 and listen again.

    Official guide: Play and record

  6. Fit the melody pitch

    Press SOUND and tap C1. Turn Y a small amount while tapping C1, and play the beat to compare.

    Check: The melody sounds comfortable with the kick and bass.

    Keep the setting that sounds best with the bass.

    Why

    In SOUND, Y is pitch. A small pitch change can make the melody fit the bass.

    Not working?

    Change Y a little at a time. If nothing fits, choose a different melody sample instead.

    Official guide: Modes

Part 4 · Make the breakdown

  1. Make a copy for the breakdown

    Stop playback and press MAIN. Press SHIFT + MAIN once. Hold MAIN and read the new number; write it down as BREAK.

    Check: You are in BREAK, and PLAY sounds exactly like MELODY. Stop.

    Why

    MELODY is finished. The breakdown starts as an exact copy of it.

    Not working?

    From now on, check the scene number before erasing anything: MELODY must stay as it is.

    Official guide: Modes

  2. Make the breakdown: clear kick and bass

    Hold MAIN first: the number must be BREAK.

    In BREAK, stopped, press MAIN and tap A. Hold ERASE + pad 1 (A1) until TRK blinks on the display, then release. Tap B and do the same with B1.

    Wrong notes gone? Press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

    Check: Press PLAY: melody, snare and hats, but no kick and no bass. Stop.

    Why

    A breakdown takes the heavy parts away so they hit harder when they return. Holding ERASE + a pad until TRK blinks clears every note of that pad in this pattern in one move.

    Not working?

    A brief ERASE press clears one step only; it is the long hold that clears the whole track. See help: “I erased or changed the wrong thing”.

    Official guide: Play and record

  3. Check all four scenes

    Hold MAIN and select INTRO, BEAT, MELODY and BREAK in turn. In each, press PLAY briefly, then stop.

    Check: INTRO: drums only. BEAT: drums, bass. MELODY: drums, bass, melody. BREAK: melody, snare, hats.

    Why

    Song mode only plays the scenes you made. If a scene is wrong now, the song will be wrong too.

    Not working?

    If one is wrong, fix it in that scene now. Your paper list of numbers is your map for the next steps. See help: “Which scene am I in?”.

    Official guide: Modes

Part 5 · Build the song list

Your song map

SectionSceneSong positionsBarsWhat you hear
IntroINTRO1–22Drums only: kick, snare and hats
Main grooveBEAT3–42Your Chapter 1 · First beat: drums and bass
MelodyMELODY5–64Drums, bass and the two-bar C melody
BreakdownBREAK72Melody, snare and hats; kick and bass removed
Melody returnMELODY8–94Kick and bass come back under the melody
EndingBREAK102The melody without kick and bass, then the song stops

Total: 16 bars. At 90 BPM in 4/4 that is about 43 seconds.

Listen to how long each song position lasts on your K.O. II. To make a section longer, give it another song position with the same scene: in the song list, SHIFT + A adds a position and SHIFT + − / + chooses its scene.

  1. Plan the song: one position, one pass

    Read the song map on this page. It uses 10 song positions: 1–2 INTRO, 3–4 BEAT, 5–6 MELODY, 7 BREAK, 8–9 MELODY, 10 BREAK.

    Check: The plan adds up to 16 bars: about 43 seconds at 90 BPM.

    Why

    Each song position plays its scene once, for as long as the scene’s longest pattern. INTRO and BEAT last one bar; MELODY and BREAK last two, because C is two bars long. To make a section longer, give it more positions.

    Not working?

    Scene numbers and song-position numbers are different things. Keep your paper list of scene numbers beside you.

    Official guide: Workflow

  2. Open the song list

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Stop playback. Hold MAIN and press ENTER to open the song list editor.

    Why

    Song mode plays the positions in order by itself.

    Not working?

    Nothing opens? Song mode needs OS 2.0 or later, and this course assumes OS 2.5.1: older OS, update first (see Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II). If this list already holds a song you care about, leave it alone and use an empty project. See help: “The song is too short or has extra positions”.

  3. Set position 1 to INTRO

    Hold SHIFT and press − / + to choose INTRO’s scene number for it.

    Check: Position 1 holds INTRO.

    You will confirm it by listening at the end.

    Why

    A new list starts with one position.

    Official guide: Modes

  4. Add position 2: INTRO

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Hold SHIFT and press A to add a position, then, still holding SHIFT, use − / + to choose its scene. Choose INTRO for position 2.

    Why

    SHIFT + A adds one position; SHIFT + − / + picks the scene for the selected position. Do not assume a new position copies the previous scene: set each one.

    Not working?

    Add while you are on the last position.

  5. Add positions 3–6

    Do the same four more times: 3 BEAT, 4 BEAT, 5 MELODY, 6 MELODY.

    Check: Release SHIFT and use − / + to step through the positions: there are exactly six.

    Not working?

    Extra position? Select it and press SHIFT + C to cut it. Wrong scene? Select the position and hold SHIFT + − / +.

    Official guide: Modes

  6. Add positions 7–10

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Add four more positions the same way: 7 BREAK, 8 MELODY, 9 MELODY, 10 BREAK. That makes nine SHIFT + A presses in total.

    Check: Release SHIFT, step through with − / +: exactly 10 positions, matching the song map.

    Why

    Repeating MELODY gives each melody section four bars; the final BREAK works as a soft ending.

    Not working?

    Lost count? Step back to position 1 with − and check each position against the song map. See help: “The song is too short or has extra positions”.

    Official guide: Modes

Part 6 · Play the whole song

  1. Play the whole song

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Press SHIFT + PLAY to play the song from the start. Listen without touching anything.

    Check: INTRO, BEAT, MELODY, BREAK, MELODY, BREAK in that order; about 43 seconds, then it stops.

    Why

    This is the moment your separate loops become one song.

    Not working?

    Much shorter than 43 seconds, or a section is missing: a position is missing or holds the wrong scene. Much longer: a pattern is longer than planned. Keeps looping: song loop is on (system setting 420 turns it off). See help: “The song is too short or has extra positions”.

    Official guide: Modes

  2. Start the song

    In the song list, press SHIFT + PLAY to start the song.

  3. A: add a little reverb

    Tap A, press FX and choose reverb with − / +. Raise the fader slightly: that is group A’s send.

    Why

    A little reverb helps drums and melody sound as if they share one room. There is one shared effect; each group only sets how much it sends.

    Not working?

    Change one thing at a time; if it sounds blurry, lower the fader. Do not hold FX while pressing a group button: that solos it.

  4. C a little too, B low

    Tap C and raise its fader a little too. Leave B’s send low.

    Check: The song sounds more finished, while the kick and bass stay clear.

    Not working?

    If the song ends, start it again.

    Official guide: Effects

  5. Your song is ready to develop

    In the song list, play the song once more with SHIFT + PLAY.

    • SHIFT + MAIN commits and moves you into a copy.
    • Hold MAIN + − / + selects a scene.
    • Brief ERASE + pad removes one note; holding ERASE + pad until TRK blinks clears a track.
    • MAIN + ENTER opens the song list; SHIFT + A adds a position; SHIFT + − / + picks its scene; SHIFT + PLAY plays from the start.

    Check: Your song has an intro, main groove, melody, breakdown, melody return and ending.

  6. Make one change from memory

    Make one change from memory, without reading this app.

    Check: You changed one thing yourself.

    For example step to the last position with + and add one more BREAK position (SHIFT + A, then SHIFT + − / +). That adds about 5 seconds.

    Not working?

    Back up the project with the official EP sample tool (computer + USB-C). To record audio, the K.O. II can send its sound over USB to a computer or phone. This app cannot capture your K.O. II. See help: “How do I export or back up?”. Next: Chapter 3 · Sample, chop & resample, where you record your own sounds.

    Official guide: Workflow
