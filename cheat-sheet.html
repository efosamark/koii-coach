Cheat sheet

The moves you learn in the first chapters, on one page to keep beside the K.O. II. It prints on its own.

01 First beat: moves

  • Navigate: stopped MAIN, − / + (SHIFT + − / + jumps a bar).
  • Write: hold RECORD + pad.
  • Erase one note: brief ERASE + pad.
  • Undo: SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.
  • Commit: SHIFT + MAIN.
  • Select a scene: hold MAIN + − / +.

From 01 / First beat, the last step.

02 Make it a song: moves

  • SHIFT + MAIN commits and moves you into a copy.
  • Hold MAIN + − / + selects a scene.
  • Brief ERASE + pad removes one note; holding ERASE + pad until TRK blinks clears a track.
  • MAIN + ENTER opens the song list; SHIFT + A adds a position; SHIFT + − / + picks its scene; SHIFT + PLAY plays from the start.

Erasing and undo

Undo is SHIFT + B while the umbrella icon is lit. Safety and erasing lists every action that erases or overwrites, and which ones have no undo.

Every chapter from 07 on has its own reference tables (pad plans, scenes, song lists) on its page, under All steps.
