Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II: moves

  • Home: press MAIN; PLAY starts and stops.
  • To the start: hold SHIFT and press − until the display shows 1.1.1.
  • Change project: hold MAIN, hold a pad 1–9 as well, then release both.
  • Browse sounds: SOUND, tap the pad, then − / + (SHIFT + + jumps ten). Hold SOUND and type a number to jump there.
  • System settings, with the unit already on: SHIFT + ERASE, three digits, ENTER, then MAIN.
  • Undo: SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

Chapter 2 · Make it a song: moves

Pad plan

  • SHIFT + MAIN commits and moves you into a copy.
  • Hold MAIN + − / + selects a scene.
  • Brief ERASE + pad removes one note; holding ERASE + pad until TRK blinks clears a track.
  • MAIN + ENTER opens the song list; SHIFT + A adds a position; SHIFT + − / + picks its scene; SHIFT + PLAY plays from the start.

Chapter 3 · Sample, chop & resample: moves and pad plan

Moves

  • Sample: tap the group, press SAMPLE, pick the source with − / +; X is the level, Y the threshold. Hold a pad to record.
  • Hands-free: hold SHIFT and press an empty pad; press SAMPLE to stop.
  • Sound edit: SOUND, tap the pad, briefly press SHIFT + SOUND; − / + steps through its pages.
  • Copy a sound: in SOUND, SHIFT + C; tap another pad, SHIFT + D.
  • Time page (tim): X chooses BPM, BAR or rev.
  • Chop: select the sample, tap the destination group, SHIFT + SAMPLE; hold the group button and − / + for EQU.
  • Resample: source rsp, hold SHIFT and press an empty pad, then PLAY.

You work in empty project 7. Each group gets one job, so you always know where a sound lives:

The official get-started guide suggests keeping group D for loops and samples. Every recording goes into the K.O. II’s shared sound library (up to 999 samples or 128 MB); a pad only points to a sample.

Chapter 4 · Play & perform: moves and section starts

Pad plan

Moves

  • Record live: at 1.1.1 tap RECORD, press PLAY, play after the count-in, then press RECORD.
  • TIMING: X sets the grid, − quantize, + free time, Y swing.
  • KEYS: select a pad, press KEYS; hold it and turn X for the key, Y for the scale.
  • Note repeat: hold TIMING and a pad; in TIMING, SHIFT + the pad latches it.
  • Punch-in FX: while playing, hold FX and press a pad.
  • Copy a pad’s notes: hold it, SHIFT + C; hold another, SHIFT + D.
  • Next scene in time: hold MAIN, − / + before the change lands.
  • Sidechain: SHIFT + FX, +; hold a group button and press a pad for the source, SHIFT + a group to dip.

This chapter uses project 6, your project from Chapters 1 and 2; Chapter 3 worked in project 7. To get back, press MAIN, stop playback, hold MAIN, hold pad 6 as well, then release both (use your own pad if you chose another project in Chapter 1). On OS 2.0+ a quick MAIN + number can select a scene instead, so press PLAY and check that you hear your own beat. Then stop.

Stop playback. Hold MAIN, use − / + until the display shows the number of the scene the section names (usually BEAT), and release. Press SHIFT + MAIN once: commit copies the scene and moves you into the copy. Hold MAIN, read the new number and write it down with the name the section gives it. The original scene stays as it was.

To change a system setting: press SHIFT + ERASE, type the three-digit code on the pads, press ENTER, then press MAIN to return. The table at the end lists every code this chapter uses and its default.

Pad plan

Moves

  • Copy a sound: in SOUND, SHIFT + C; tap another pad, SHIFT + D.

Chapter 6 · Tech house: destination, scenes and song list

Pad plan

Moves

  • Load by number: hold SOUND, type the number on the pads, release.
  • Double a pattern: arm RECORD, hold SHIFT and press +, then disarm.
  • A fresh pattern for a group: tap it, then SHIFT + A.
  • Sidechain: SHIFT + FX, +; hold a group button and press a pad for the source, SHIFT + a group to duck.
  • Sections: SHIFT + MAIN, then clear what you don’t need with ERASE + the pad until TRK blinks.
  • Reverb: tap the group, FX, − / + to reverb, raise the fader.
  • Song list: hold MAIN and press ENTER; SHIFT + A adds a position, SHIFT + − / + picks its scene.

128 BPM, 4/4, 16 grid
Main loop
4 bars / 64 steps
Roles
A drums · B bass · C vocal hook and synth stab · D optional riser

The secret is not maximum sounds all the time. Kick and bass repeat to make people dance. Changes happen at the ends of bars, between sections, and around the vocal.

Chapter 7 · Vocal house: sound roles and song map

Pad plan

Moves

  • Load a sample by number: SOUND, tap the pad, hold SOUND, type the number, release.
  • Trim: briefly SHIFT + SOUND, − / + to the trim page; X start, Y length.
  • Play to the end: on the sound page, turn X to oneshot.
  • Reverb on a group: tap it, FX, − / + to reverb, raise the fader.
  • Grow and strip: SHIFT + MAIN, then hold ERASE + the pad until TRK blinks to clear one sound.
  • Play it in: tap RECORD, press PLAY, play after the count-in.
  • Song list: hold MAIN and press ENTER; SHIFT + A adds a position, SHIFT + − / + picks its scene.

The same roles as in Learn: A drums, B bass, D your own samples. Group C stays empty.

A1
Kick (factory 1–99)
A2
Clap or snare (100–199)
A3
Closed hat (200–299)
A4
Open hat (200–299) or percussion (300–399)
B1
Bass (400–499)
D1 · V1
Main hook: two to four words, under about 1.9 s (one bar at 128 BPM)
D2 · V2
Short answer: one word, about half a second
D3 · V3
Tiny chop: one syllable or a breath, about 0.2 s
D4 · V4
Texture: a long hum, “ooh” or breath, 2–4 s
D5 · V5
Second phrase: a variation of the hook, under about 1.9 s

A dance track needs a clear low end and a clear voice. Let B1 carry the bass and keep the vocals above it. The voice is the feature, not the kick, clap or hat.
