Your K.O. II’s OS version

Choose the OS version on your K.O. II. Steps that need a newer one show a “Needs OS …+” badge. The chapters are written for OS 2.5.x, the latest release.

Official guide: What’s new

While practising

Keep the screen on while practising

Auto keeps the screen awake while you work through the steps, so a propped-up phone stays readable. Off leaves it to your device’s screen timeout.

Keyboard shortcuts

With a keyboard: / search, M menu, T the Tools sheet in a chapter, ? the full list. Off frees the letter keys for screen readers and voice control. Ctrl + K and the arrow keys in a chapter keep working.

Text size in the steps

Larger makes each step’s moves bigger, for reading at arm’s length. Your browser’s text size still applies on top.

Done · Next button

On the left puts Done · Next under your left thumb, with Tools and Back to its right.

Your progress

Progress stays in this browser.

There is no cloud sync. Clearing browser data, or a phone’s storage clean-up, can remove your progress. Save a progress file now and then: it covers every chapter.

Reset settings restores the defaults: screen Auto, shortcuts On, text Default, Done · Next on the right, step by step in every chapter, set-up not answered. Your progress and OS choice stay.

Offline and version

Offline use needs one full visit to the online site first. External guides still need internet.
