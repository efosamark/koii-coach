K.O. II / Coach

Chapter 5 · About 2–3 hours

Full hip-hop beat

Build a 64-bar hip-hop beat around a phrase you sample yourself.

Old-school hip-hop at 90 BPM: your Chapter 1 drums and bass, a few sung words you sample yourself, a bare break and a twist near the end. The song list plays it all back in about 2 minutes 51 seconds.

You will make: a 64-bar hip-hop beat around a phrase you sampled, in project 8.

Before you start

  • Empty project 8
  • Headphones or speakers
  • A phone and a cable, or the mic
  • Paper and a pencil
Written for OS 2.5.1. On an older OS, update first. Finish Chapter 1 · First beat and Chapter 2 · Make it a song before you start. Chapter 3 · Sample, chop & resample and Chapter 4 · Play & perform go deeper into sampling and live playing.

What you need

Headphones or speakers on the K.O. II’s output, and one source for the sung phrase:

• A phone or computer on a USB-C cable.
• The headphone or line output of a phone, laptop or turntable, on a stereo 3.5 mm cable into the K.O. II’s 3.5 mm input.
• No cable: the built-in mic and your voice.

Turntable? It needs an RIAA amplifier or a built-in amp. If it has a PHONO/LINE switch, as the Audio-Technica LP60X family does, set it to LINE. See Audio-Technica’s LP60X setup guide.

Your pad plan

Part 1 · Start a fresh project and rebuild your beat

Goal: rebuild the Chapter 1 drums in project 8 at 90 BPM, with the snare played live.

  1. Open empty project 8

    Press MAIN. If anything plays, press PLAY to stop it. Hold MAIN, hold pad 8 as well, then release both.

    Check: Project 8 opens: the display shows P 8.

    Why

    Chapters 1–4 use projects 6 and 7, and 9 is your scratch project from Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II. That leaves 8 free.

  2. Check that project 8 is empty

    Press PLAY, then stop. Tap A, then A1, A2 and A3.

    Check: Nothing sounds: PLAY and the pads are silent.

    Why

    Your Chapter 2 song stays untouched in project 6, with its pads and song list.

    Not working?

    A pad makes a sound? You may still be in another project: a quick MAIN + number tap selects a scene instead. Hold pad 8 until the display shows P 8. Project 8 holds work you want to keep? Back it up first with the EP sample tool: the sounds you load next replace its pads. See help “My project is not empty”.

  3. A1: load the kick

    Press SOUND, tap A, then tap A1. Hold SOUND, type 1 on the pads and release SOUND: A1 loads sample 001.

    Check: The display shows the sample number for A1.

  4. A2 and A3: load snare and hat

    Tap A2, hold SOUND, type 1, 0, 0 and release (sample 100). Tap A3, hold SOUND, type 2, 0, 0 and release (sample 200).

    Check: The display shows sample numbers 100, then 200.

    Why

    The same sounds as Chapter 1. Factory kicks sit at 1–99, snares at 100–199 and hi-hats at 200–299.

  5. Listen, and set the hat lower

    Tap A1, A2 and A3. With A3 selected, turn X down until the hat is clearly quieter than the kick.

    Check: A low kick, a sharp snare, a short bright tick.

    Not working?

    A pad silent, or a long sound? Browse that range with − / + and keep a short one.

  6. Set the tempo to 90 BPM

    Press TEMPO and turn X until the display shows 90. Or hold TEMPO and type 9, 0.

    Check: The display shows 90, the new tempo in BPM.

    Why

    At 90 BPM one bar lasts about 2.7 seconds.

  7. Set the grid to 16, quantized

    Press TIMING and turn X until it shows 16 (not 16T). Press − to choose quantize. Press MAIN.

    Check: The MAIN icon is lit again.

    Leave Y (swing) alone.

  8. Go to 1.1.1 with group A

    Stopped, in MAIN, with A selected: hold SHIFT and press − until the display shows 1.1.1.

    Check: The display shows 1.1.1: the start of bar 1.

  9. A1: write the four kicks

    Hold RECORD, tap A1, release. Use + to reach 1.2.3, 1.3.1 and 1.4.3 and write A1 the same way at each.

    Check: A1 lights at all four positions.

    Why

    A lit pad means a note is written on that step.

  10. A3: write the hats

    Still stopped in group A, hold RECORD and tap A3 at every .1 and .3: 1.1.1, 1.1.3, 1.2.1, 1.2.3, 1.3.1, 1.3.3, 1.4.1 and 1.4.3.

    Check: Press PLAY. Kick and steady hats.

    Press PLAY to stop.

    Why

    Some of these already hold a kick. A hat there too is correct.

  11. Practise the snare on 2 and 4

    Press PLAY and, without touching RECORD, tap A2 on beats two and four. Count 1, TWO, 3, FOUR.

    Check: Each tap plays a snare over the beat.

    Press PLAY to stop.

    Why

    Now play the snare live.

  12. Arm recording with a count-in

    Tap and release RECORD, then press PLAY.

    Check: A four-beat count-in plays, then recording starts.

  13. Play the snare on two and four

    After the count-in, tap A2 on two and four. Then press RECORD to stop recording.

    Check: Your snare now repeats by itself each loop.

    Listen, then press PLAY to stop.

    Why

    Quantize snaps your taps to the 16-step grid.

  14. Checkpoint: the rebuilt beat

    Checkpoint. Stopped, step to 1.2.1 and 1.4.1: A2 lights at both and nowhere else. Press PLAY.

    Check: You hear the Chapter 1 · First beat drums.

    Stop.

    Hear the beat
    Why

    Kick on 1, the “and” of 2, 3 and the “and” of 4. Snare on 2 and 4, hats on every eighth note.

    Not working?

    Press SHIFT + B (undo) while the umbrella is lit and record the snare again. Or remove a stray note with a brief ERASE + its pad at its position, then write A2 at 1.2.1 and 1.4.1 with RECORD held.

Checkpoint: project 8 plays the Chapter 1 drums: the kick pattern, the snare on 2 and 4, and steady hats.

If it doesn’t: a stray or missing snare? Press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit and record it again. Pads played sounds before you loaded any? See Check that project 8 is empty.

Part 2 · Add the bass and get ready for the vocal

Goal: add two bass pulses on B1, keep the scene as VERSE and copy it as HOOK for the vocal.

  1. B1: load a bass

    Tap B. Press SOUND, tap B1, hold SOUND, type 4, 0, 0 and release: B1 loads sample 400.

    Check: Tap B1: one low bass note.

    Factory basses sit at 400–499. Turn X down if it booms over the kick.

  2. B1: write two bass pulses

    Press MAIN. Stopped, with B selected, go to 1.1.1, hold RECORD, tap B1 and release. Do the same at 1.3.1.

    Check: B1 lights at both positions.

  3. Hear the bass with the kick

    Press PLAY.

    Check: The bass joins the kick on beats 1 and 3.

    Stop.

  4. Make C and D two bars long

    Tap C. Hold RECORD, press + once, then release both. Tap D and do the same.

    Check: The display shows Ln.2 for C, then for D.

    Why

    A and B stay one bar and repeat under the two-bar C and D, which stay empty for now.

  5. Optional: open timing correct

    Needs OS 2.5 or newer

    Stopped, tap A, then press SHIFT + TIMING to open timing correct.

    Check: The display changes as timing correct opens.

  6. Optional: shift the snare later

    Needs OS 2.5 or newer

    Hold A2 and press + three times, then release and press MAIN. Press PLAY.

    Check: The snare sits a hair behind the beat, a laid-back hip-hop feel.

    Stop.

    Why

    Each press moves every A2 snare one tick (1/96 of a beat) later.

    Not working?

    Too much? Open timing correct again and tap A2 once: a tap there snaps all its notes back to the grid.

  7. Name VERSE and commit

    Hold MAIN and write the scene number down as VERSE. Release MAIN and press SHIFT + MAIN once.

    Check: VERSE’s scene number is on your paper.

  8. Name the copy HOOK

    Hold MAIN again. Write the new number as HOOK.

    Check: The number has changed: you are in the copy.

    Why

    You build the vocal in HOOK. VERSE stays as it is.

  9. Checkpoint: HOOK sounds like VERSE

    Checkpoint. In HOOK press PLAY.

    Check: Drums plus two bass pulses per bar, exactly like VERSE.

    Stop.

    Hear the beat
    Not working?

    No bass? Tap B and check that B1 lights at 1.1.1 and 1.3.1, and that B1 makes a sound in SOUND. The scene number did not change? Press SHIFT + MAIN once more and read the number again.

Checkpoint: HOOK sounds exactly like VERSE: the drums plus two bass pulses per bar.

If it doesn’t: no bass? Check that B1 lights at 1.1.1 and 1.3.1 in group B. Same scene number? Press SHIFT + MAIN once more.

Part 3 · Choose your source and the vocal phrase

Goal: connect one source, choose a PHRASE and an ANSWER word, and get SAMPLE ready on group C.

  1. Turn down, then set up one source

    Turn your source and your headphones down, then set up one source.

    Check: USB lights the USB MIDI icon. A cable or the mic shows nothing yet.

    USB: connect a phone or computer with a USB-C cable and choose the K.O. II as its sound output. Line: a stereo 3.5 mm cable from the source’s output into the K.O. II’s 3.5 mm input, not its output, MIDI or SYNC socket. Mic: connect nothing.

  2. Find your PHRASE

    Play your song and find two to four sung words you like, one to two seconds long, ideally with quiet music behind them.

    Using the mic? Choose two to four words you will sing or say.

    Why

    Sampling a finished song records the backing music too. The K.O. II does not separate the voice.

  3. Note where the PHRASE is

    This is your PHRASE. Write down where it is, for example the time shown on your phone.

  4. Pick your ANSWER word

    Pick one short word or syllable from inside the PHRASE as your ANSWER, ideally half a second or less, and write it next to PHRASE.

    Why

    You record the phrase once. Later you copy it to a second pad and cut that copy down to the ANSWER.

  5. C: open SAMPLE

    Tap C, then press SAMPLE.

    Check: The pads blink, ready to record.

    Do not press a pad yet.

  6. Choose the input and level

    Press − / + until the display shows your input: usb, in or mic. Play the song and turn X up until it sounds strong, not harsh.

    Check: The display names your input: usb, in or mic.

    Why

    Skip rsp (resample) and L.IN / R.IN (one side of the line input). Line in and USB come in mono and stereo: mono is fine for a voice and allows samples up to 40 seconds.

  7. Set the threshold low

    Turn Y (threshold) low so recording starts as soon as you hold a pad.

    Check: The pads still blink, ready to record.

  8. Checkpoint: hear your source

    Checkpoint. Play the song.

    Check: Cable: you hear the source as you turn X. Mic: the MIC icon lights.

    Not working?

    Check the input on the display, that the cable is in the K.O. II’s input and that the source volume is up. On a turntable, set LINE. USB: make the K.O. II the phone’s sound output. Still silent? System setting 511 turns on live monitoring of the USB input: with the K.O. II already on, press SHIFT + ERASE, type 5, 1, 1, press ENTER, then MAIN. 510 is the default. Never hold SHIFT + ERASE while switching the K.O. II on: that formats the drive, and factory sounds cannot be recovered. Mic: the MIC icon lights while the mic is in use. Record a test word to hear it.

Checkpoint: your source plays, the input level is set, and the PHRASE and ANSWER are on your paper.

If it doesn’t: check the input on the display, the cable and the source volume. Still silent? See Not working? in Checkpoint: hear your source.

Part 4 · Record the phrase once and trim it

Goal: record the PHRASE on C1, copy it to C2, then trim away the extra audio: C1 keeps the whole phrase, C2 only the ANSWER.

  1. Start the song before the PHRASE

    Still in SAMPLE with C selected, start the song a few seconds before the PHRASE.

    Check: The pads still blink while the song plays.

  2. C1: record the PHRASE

    Right before the first word, hold C1. Keep holding through the words and release right after the last one.

    Check: C1 stops blinking and stays lit: it holds your sample.

    Not working?

    Need both hands for the source? Sample hands-free: hold SHIFT and press C1 right before the words, then press SAMPLE right after them.

  3. Listen to C1

    Press MAIN, tap C and tap C1.

    Check: You hear your phrase.

    Why

    A little extra audio at either end is fine. You trim it next.

    Not working?

    Not happy? Press SAMPLE and record C1 again by holding it.

  4. Copy C1’s sound

    Press SHIFT + C only once: twice copies every sound in the group.

    Press SOUND, tap C, then tap C1 to select it. Press SHIFT + C once to copy its sound.

    Check: Selecting C1 plays your phrase.

  5. Paste it onto C2

    Tap C2, then press SHIFT + D to paste.

    Check: Tap C2: it plays the same phrase.

  6. C1: open the trim page

    Tap C1 to select it. Briefly press SHIFT + SOUND to open sound edit, then press − / + until the display shows the trim page (TRI).

    Check: The display shows TRI: C1’s trim page.

  7. Trim the start of the PHRASE

    Turn X (start) a little at a time, tapping C1 between changes, until playback starts right before the first word.

    Check: C1 now starts right before the first word.

    Do not cut into the word. Hold SHIFT while turning for finer steps.

    Why

    Trim changes how this pad plays in this project. The recording itself is untouched.

  8. Trim the end of the PHRASE

    Never hold SHIFT + SOUND on this page: it crops the recording on disk for good.

    Stay on the trim page. Turn Y (length) down, tapping C1, until playback stops right after the LAST word.

    Check: Every word stays clear, and playback ends soon after the last one.

    Why

    C2, and later D1, play the same recording, so a crop would cut them too. One bar at 90 BPM lasts about 2.7 seconds: keep the phrase shorter.

  9. C2: open the trim page

    Press SOUND, tap C2, briefly press SHIFT + SOUND and go to the trim page again.

    Check: The display shows TRI again, for C2.

  10. Trim C2 to the ANSWER

    Turn X until playback starts at your ANSWER word, then turn Y until it stops right after it: about half a second or less.

    Check: Tap C1, then C2: phrase, then only the answer word.

  11. C2: set oneshot

    Still editing C2, press − / + to the page where X sets the play mode, and turn X to oneshot.

    Check: The display shows one (oneshot), not key or leg.

  12. C1: set oneshot

    Do the same for C1: press SOUND, tap C1, briefly press SHIFT + SOUND, find that page, turn X to oneshot.

    Check: The display shows one for C1 too.

    Why

    Oneshot plays the whole trimmed sound from one tap.

  13. Set the vocal volume

    Press MAIN, then SOUND. Set C1 and C2 so the words are clear but not louder than the snare. Press MAIN.

    Check: The words sound clear, no louder than the snare.

    Leave Y (pitch) alone.

    Why

    Here X is the selected pad’s volume.

  14. Checkpoint: the trimmed pads

    Checkpoint. In MAIN tap C, then C1 and C2.

    Check: The whole phrase, first word to last, then only the ANSWER word.

    Not working?

    A word is clipped? On the trim page, turn X down or Y up. A different sound? Pressing − / + in SOUND changes the pad’s sample: press them back until your phrase returns. Harsh or crackly? Record C1 again with a lower X in SAMPLE, then copy it to C2 again.

Checkpoint: C1 plays the whole phrase and C2 only the ANSWER word.

If it doesn’t: a word is clipped? On the trim page, turn X down or Y up. A different sound? Press − / + in SOUND back until your phrase returns.

Part 5 · Put the phrase and answer into HOOK

Goal: in HOOK, place the PHRASE at 1.1.1 and the ANSWER at 2.3.1.

  1. C1: place the PHRASE at 1.1.1

    Hold MAIN and check the number is HOOK.

    Stopped, in MAIN, tap C. Hold SHIFT and press − until the display shows 1.1.1. Hold RECORD, tap C1, release.

    Check: C1 lights at 1.1.1.

    Why

    This writes the full phrase at the start of bar 1. Tapping a pad without RECORD only auditions it.

  2. Check the phrase timing

    Press PLAY, listen to one two-bar pass, then stop.

    Check: The phrase starts right at the top of bar 1, with room after it.

    Why

    Quantize snaps the start of the sample to the grid. It does not move the words inside the recording.

    Not working?

    First word late? Press SOUND, tap C, tap C1, briefly press SHIFT + SOUND and go to the trim page (TRI). Turn X a little at a time until playback starts right on the first word, then press MAIN. In plain SOUND, X is volume, not trim.

  3. Jump to bar 2

    Stopped, with C selected: press SHIFT + + once to jump to bar 2.

    Check: The display shows 2.1.1.

    Not working?

    SHIFT + − / + jumps bars only while RECORD is not armed and no pad is held.

  4. Go to 2.3.1

    Press + until it shows 2.3.1: bar 2, beat 3.

    Check: The display shows 2.3.1.

  5. C2: place the ANSWER

    Hold RECORD, tap C2, release.

    Check: The ANSWER now lands in bar 2, after the phrase.

  6. Check the vocal pattern

    Step through C with − / +.

    Check: Only two notes light, C1 at 1.1.1 and C2 at 2.3.1.

    Why

    Count it: full phrase in bar 1, space, short answer in bar 2.

    Not working?

    If the phrase still runs into the answer, choose a shorter phrase rather than cutting a word in half.

  7. Checkpoint: drums with the vocal

    Checkpoint. Press PLAY for two full passes, then stop.

    Check: Drums and bass under the phrase in bar 1, the answer in bar 2.

    Why

    The sampled backing music keeps its own tempo and will not follow 90 BPM. If it fights the drums, choose a phrase with less music behind it.

    Not working?

    No vocal? Check that you are in HOOK and that C was selected while you wrote. An extra note? Go to it and remove it with a brief ERASE + its pad.

Checkpoint: HOOK plays the drums with the whole phrase in bar 1 and the answer in bar 2.

If it doesn’t: the phrase starts late? Trim its start, as in Check the phrase timing. It runs into the answer? Choose a shorter phrase rather than cutting a word in half.

Part 6 · Make the bass fit the voice

Goal: make every word clear: change the bass if it clashes, and keep the hats below the voice.

  1. Listen to the bass with the words

    Press PLAY and listen.

    Check: Drums, bass, phrase and answer play together.

  2. Retune the bass if it clashes

    Change the bass, not the singer: leave C1 and C2 at their original pitch.

    If it clashes, stop, press SOUND, tap B, tap B1 and turn Y (pitch) a little, then play again.

    Check: B1 plays the bass at a new pitch.

    Why

    Pad settings belong to the whole project, so VERSE changes too.

  3. Nothing fits? Try another bass

    In SOUND with B1 selected, browse other bass sounds in 400–499 with − / +.

    Check: The sample number changes with each press.

  4. HOOK only: drop the bass

    Hold MAIN first: the number must be HOOK.

    Clears every note of B1 in HOOK.

    Still clashing? Stopped, tap B, hold ERASE + B1 until TRK blinks, then release.

    Wrong pad? Press SHIFT + B at once, while the umbrella is lit.

    Check: Press PLAY: HOOK plays without its bass. Stop.

    Drums plus a good vocal can work without bass.

  5. Lower the hats if they cover the words

    In MAIN, X sets the input level, not pad volume.

    In SOUND, tap A, tap A3 and lower X if the hats cover the words.

    Check: The hats sit below the words.

    Make one change, listen, stop.

  6. Set the vocal pads

    Tap C and set C1 and C2 so the words come through clearly.

    Check: Every word of the phrase and answer is clear.

  7. Keep the bass below the kick

    Tap B, tap B1 and keep it below the kick.

    Check: The kick stands out above the bass.

  8. Checkpoint: play HOOK

    Checkpoint. Play HOOK.

    Check: Drums, bass if you kept it, phrase and answer, every word clear.

  9. Play VERSE

    Hold MAIN, select VERSE and play.

    Check: Drums and bass, no vocal.

    Stop.

    Not working?

    VERSE lost its bass? You cleared it in the wrong scene: press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit, or write B1 again at 1.1.1 and 1.3.1 in VERSE’s group B. Words buried? Lower the hats and the bass in SOUND rather than raising the vocal.

Checkpoint: every word is clear in HOOK, and VERSE still plays drums and bass.

If it doesn’t: VERSE lost its bass? Press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit, or write B1 again at 1.1.1 and 1.3.1 in VERSE. Words buried? Lower the hats and bass in SOUND rather than raising the vocal.

Part 7 · Make the intro and the break

Goal: copy VERSE as INTRO without the bass, then INTRO as BREAK: your kicks with two hats per bar.

  1. Copy VERSE as INTRO

    Hold MAIN, select VERSE with − / +, release. Press SHIFT + MAIN, then hold MAIN and write the new number as INTRO.

    Check: The new number differs from VERSE’s.

  2. Clear INTRO’s bass

    Hold MAIN first: the number must be INTRO.

    Clears every note of B1 in INTRO.

    Stopped, tap B, hold ERASE + B1 until TRK blinks, then release.

    Wrong pad? Press SHIFT + B at once, while the umbrella is lit.

    Check: Tap B1: it still plays its bass sound.

  3. Play INTRO

    Press PLAY.

    Check: INTRO is drums only.

    Stop.

    Hear the beat

  4. Check VERSE and HOOK are unchanged

    Hold MAIN, select VERSE and release. Press PLAY, then stop. Do the same with HOOK.

    Check: VERSE still has its bass, and HOOK its vocal.

    Why

    Copies are independent.

    Not working?

    If an earlier scene changed, press SHIFT + B at once while the umbrella is lit.

  5. Copy INTRO as BREAK

    Hold MAIN, select INTRO again and release. Press SHIFT + MAIN once and write the new number as BREAK.

    Check: The new number differs from INTRO’s.

  6. Clear the snare in BREAK

    Hold MAIN first: the number must be BREAK.

    Clears every note of A2 in BREAK.

    In BREAK, stopped, tap A. Hold ERASE + A2 until TRK blinks, then release.

    Wrong pad? Press SHIFT + B at once, while the umbrella is lit.

    Check: Press PLAY: kicks and hats, no snare. Stop.

  7. Clear the hats in BREAK

    Still in BREAK: hold MAIN to check the number.

    Clears every note of A3 in BREAK.

    Still in group A, hold ERASE + A3 until TRK blinks to clear the hats.

    Wrong pad? Press SHIFT + B at once, while the umbrella is lit.

    Check: Press PLAY: only the kicks are left. Stop.

  8. A3: two hats per bar

    Go to 1.1.1 and write A3 with RECORD held, and again at 1.3.1: two hats per bar, on beats 1 and 3.

    Check: A3 lights at 1.1.1 and 1.3.1 only.

  9. Checkpoint: play BREAK

    Checkpoint. Play BREAK.

    Check: Your kick rhythm with two hats per bar, and no snare, bass or vocal.

    Stop.

    Hear the beat
    Why

    It should feel empty: that makes the return feel bigger.

    Not working?

    Hold MAIN and check the number is BREAK. INTRO lost its snare or hats? You cleared them there: select INTRO and press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit. Or write them again: snare at 1.2.1 and 1.4.1, hats on every .1 and .3.

Checkpoint: INTRO plays drums only, BREAK plays kicks and two hats per bar, and VERSE and HOOK are unchanged.

If it doesn’t: hold MAIN and check the scene number. INTRO lost its snare or hats? You cleared them there: select INTRO and press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

Part 8 · Add the twist and the ending

Goal: copy HOOK as RETURN with a short word twice near the end of bar 2, and INTRO as END with one kick per bar.

  1. Copy HOOK as RETURN

    Hold MAIN and select HOOK. Play it once to check, then stop. Press SHIFT + MAIN and write the new number as RETURN.

    Check: The new number differs from HOOK’s.

    Why

    Only RETURN gets the twist.

  2. Copy C1’s sound

    Press SOUND, tap C, tap C1 and press SHIFT + C once.

    Check: Selecting C1 plays your trimmed phrase.

  3. Paste it onto D1

    Tap D, tap D1 and press SHIFT + D.

    Check: Tap D1: it plays your phrase.

  4. D1: open the trim page

    With D1 selected, briefly press SHIFT + SOUND and go to the trim page.

    Check: The display shows TRI, now for D1.

  5. Trim D1 to one short word

    Set X and Y so only one short word or syllable plays, about 0.2 seconds. On the play-mode page turn X to oneshot.

    Check: D1 plays only one short word.

  6. Make D1 quieter

    Press MAIN, then SOUND, and lower D1’s X so it is a small detail.

    Check: D1 now plays quieter, a small detail.

  7. Optional: open D1’s time page

    Needs OS 2.5 or newer

    With D1 selected, briefly press SHIFT + SOUND and press − / + until the display shows tim.

    Check: The display shows tim.

  8. Optional: reverse the word

    Needs OS 2.5 or newer

    Turn X until it shows rev, then tap D1. Press MAIN.

    Check: The word plays backwards.

    Prefer it forwards? Turn X back to where it was.

    Not working?

    If it plays a different part of the recording, check D1’s trim.

  9. Go to 2.4.1 in RETURN

    Hold MAIN and check the number is RETURN.

    Stopped, tap D. Go to 1.1.1, press SHIFT + + to jump to bar 2, then + until the display shows 2.4.1.

    Check: The display shows 2.4.1: bar 2, beat 4.

  10. D1: write the word twice

    Hold RECORD, tap D1, release. Press + twice to reach 2.4.3 and write D1 again. Play two passes.

    Check: The word repeats twice near the end of bar 2.

  11. Copy INTRO as END

    Hold MAIN, select INTRO, release. Press SHIFT + MAIN and write the new number as END.

    Check: The new number differs from INTRO’s.

  12. Clear the kicks, write one per bar

    Hold MAIN first: the number must be END.

    Clears every note of A1 in END.

    Stopped, tap A and hold ERASE + A1 until TRK blinks. Go to 1.1.1 and write A1 with RECORD held.

    Wrong pad? Press SHIFT + B at once, while the umbrella is lit.

    Check: Press PLAY: one kick per bar under the snare and hats. Stop.

  13. Checkpoint: play RETURN and HOOK

    Checkpoint. Play RETURN, then HOOK.

    Check: RETURN repeats the word twice near the end of bar 2. HOOK does not.

    Not working?

    The word also plays in HOOK? In HOOK, tap D and hold ERASE + D1 until TRK blinks. The two hits cut each other off? Shorten D1 a little more on the trim page, or remove the note at 2.4.3 with a brief ERASE + D1.

  14. Play END

    Play END.

    Check: One kick per bar with snare and hats, no bass, no vocal.

    Stop.

    Hear the beat

Checkpoint: RETURN adds the short word twice, HOOK has no extra word, and END has one kick per bar.

If it doesn’t: the word also plays in HOOK? See Not working? in Checkpoint: play RETURN and HOOK. The two hits cut each other off? Shorten D1 a little more on the trim page.

Part 9 · Set the section lengths and build the song

Goal: set each section’s length with group C, then build the song list: INTRO, VERSE, HOOK, BREAK, RETURN and END.

  1. Read the song map

    Read the song map below and keep your six scene numbers beside you.

    Why

    A song position plays its scene once, for as long as its longest pattern. A and B are one bar and repeat, so C sets each section’s length.

  2. INTRO: select group C

    Hold MAIN and select INTRO. Stopped, tap C.

    Check: The scene number matches INTRO on your paper.

  3. Double C in INTRO

    Tap RECORD to arm it, hold SHIFT and press + once, release SHIFT, then tap RECORD again to disarm.

    Check: The display shows Ln.4: C’s pattern is four bars long.

    Why

    This doubles the pattern’s length and copies its notes into the new bars.

  4. Do the same in END

    Do the same in END.

    Check: The display shows Ln.4 in END too.

  5. Make BREAK’s C eight bars

    Select BREAK. Tap C, arm RECORD, hold SHIFT and press + twice: 2 → 4 → 8 bars. Disarm RECORD.

    Check: The display shows Ln.8: C’s pattern is eight bars long.

  6. Make VERSE, HOOK and RETURN 16 bars

    Do the same in VERSE, HOOK and RETURN, pressing + three times in each: 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 bars.

    Check: Each press doubles the length shown, up to 16 bars.

    Why

    In HOOK and RETURN the vocal notes are copied along, so the phrase still plays every two bars.

    Not working?

    Doubled the wrong group? Press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

  7. Open the song list

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Stopped, hold MAIN and press the ENTER pad to open the song list.

    Check: The display shows the current song position.

    Why

    This project’s list starts with one position.

  8. Set position 1 to INTRO

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Hold SHIFT and press − / + until position 1 holds INTRO’s scene number.

    Check: Position 1 holds INTRO’s scene number.

  9. Add VERSE as position 2

    Hold SHIFT and press A to add a position. Still holding SHIFT, use − / + to set its scene to VERSE.

    Check: Position 2 holds VERSE’s scene number.

  10. Add HOOK, BREAK, RETURN and END

    Add while you are on the last position.

    Add four more the same way: HOOK, BREAK, RETURN, END. Release SHIFT and step through with − / +.

    Check: Exactly six positions, in the song map’s order.

    Not working?

    An extra position? Select it and press SHIFT + C to cut it.

  11. Checkpoint: play the song

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Checkpoint. Press SHIFT + PLAY to play the song from the start, and listen without touching anything.

    Check: INTRO, VERSE, HOOK, BREAK, RETURN and END, about 2:51, then it stops.

    Not working?

    Much shorter? A scene’s C is still two bars, or a position is missing. Much longer? A pattern is longer than planned: check C’s length in that scene. It keeps looping? Song loop is on: system setting 420 turns it off.

Checkpoint: the song plays INTRO, VERSE, HOOK, BREAK, RETURN and END, about 2:51, then stops.

If it doesn’t: much shorter? A scene’s C is still two bars, or a position is missing. It keeps looping? Song loop is on: system setting 420 turns it off.

Part 10 · Finish, perform and keep your work

Goal: balance the song, record it and back up the project.

  1. Play the song from the beginning

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Open the song list: hold MAIN and press ENTER. Press SHIFT + PLAY to play the song from the start at a comfortable volume.

    Check: The song starts with INTRO: drums only.

  2. Listen to the balance

    Listen for kick and snare first, then the bass, then whether the words are clear.

    Check: Kick and snare stand out, and every word is clear.

    Stop and change only one pad volume in SOUND if needed.

    Why

    Pad settings affect every scene, so listen through again.

  3. Optional: start the song

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    In the song list, press SHIFT + PLAY.

    Check: The song plays from the start, INTRO first.

  4. Optional: add reverb to the vocal

    Do not hold RECORD while moving the fader: that records your fader moves.

    Tap C, press FX and choose reverb with − / +, then raise the fader a little. Leave B’s reverb low. Press MAIN when done.

    Check: The vocal gains a light reverb tail.

    Why

    In FX, the fader sets how much of group C goes to the reverb.

    Not working?

    Recorded fader moves by accident? Stopped, hold ERASE + FADER until FDR blinks to clear them.

  5. Listen to BREAK into RETURN

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Open the song list: hold MAIN and press ENTER. Select position 4, BREAK, with − / + and press PLAY.

    Check: After the bare break, the phrase, answer and repeated word sound bigger.

    Change one thing at a time.

    Why

    The song plays on from there.

    Not working?

    If the repeated word distracts, stop, press MAIN, hold MAIN to select RETURN, tap D and hold ERASE + D1 until TRK blinks.

  6. Optional: scene changes wait for the bar (411)

    With the K.O. II already on.

    Press SHIFT + ERASE (system settings), type 4, 1, 1, press ENTER, then MAIN.

    Check: The SYSTEM icon lights, then goes out after MAIN.

    Why

    With 411, a scene you pick starts at the end of the current bar, so you can play the song by hand.

  7. Optional: start INTRO by hand

    Press MAIN to leave the song list, hold MAIN to select INTRO, and press PLAY.

    Check: INTRO plays: drums only.

  8. Optional: pick each next scene in time

    In the last bar of each section, hold MAIN and press + or − to pick the next scene, following the song map’s bar counts.

    Check: Each new scene starts at the bar’s end.

    Hold FX and press pads for punch-in effects. Chapter 4 · Play & perform has more.

  9. Optional: instant scene changes again (410)

    Afterwards set 410 the same way to switch scenes instantly again.

    Check: The SYSTEM icon lights, then goes out after MAIN.

  10. Connect a recorder

    Needs OS 2.5 or newer

    Connect the K.O. II to a computer or phone with USB-C and choose the K.O. II as the recording app’s input.

    Check: Your recording app lists the K.O. II as an input.

    That is USB audio out. Or plug the K.O. II’s output into a recorder or audio interface.

    Why

    Record your song.

  11. Record the song

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Start recording there, then press SHIFT + PLAY in the song list.

    Check: The song plays while your app records.

    Why

    Commit copies scenes. It does not save audio.

  12. Write down your scene numbers

    Write the project number and the INTRO, VERSE, HOOK, BREAK, RETURN and END scene numbers on paper.

  13. Back up the project

    Back up the project to a computer with the EP sample tool.

    Check: A new backup file is on your computer.

    Releasing a track that samples someone else’s record? Get permission first: sampling does not grant rights.

    Why

    Your ticks are saved only in this browser.

  14. Checkpoint: the finished song

    Needs OS 2.0 or newer

    Checkpoint. In the song list, play the song once more with SHIFT + PLAY.

    Check: You hear a finished beat of about 2:51.

    Why

    A short drum intro, the groove, the hook with your phrase and answer, a bare break, the return with the twist and a short ending.

    Not working?

    Find the one scene that sounds wrong, hold MAIN to select it, fix it there, then play the song again.

  15. Your hip-hop moves

    Read the seven moves below, then try one from memory.

    • Sample: tap C, press SAMPLE, pick the input with − / +; X level, Y threshold; hold the pad.
    • Copy a sound: in SOUND, SHIFT + C; tap another pad, SHIFT + D.
    • Trim: briefly SHIFT + SOUND, − / + to the trim page; X start, Y length.
    • New section: SHIFT + MAIN, then hold MAIN and write the number down.
    • Clear a pad’s notes: stopped, hold ERASE + the pad until TRK blinks.
    • Double a pattern: arm RECORD, hold SHIFT and press +, then disarm.
    • Song list: hold MAIN and press ENTER; SHIFT + A adds a position, SHIFT + − / + picks its scene.

Checkpoint: you have a recording of the finished song, your scene numbers on paper and a backup of the project.

If it doesn’t: find the one scene that sounds wrong, hold MAIN to select it, fix it there, then play the song again.
