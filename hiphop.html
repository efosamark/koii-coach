CHAPTER 09 / ABOUT 2–3 HOURS

From a sampled voice to a full hip-hop beat

Build a 64-bar old-school hip-hop beat in a fresh project: the drums and bass from 01 First beat, a few sung words you sample yourself, a stripped-down break and a repeated-word twist near the end. Six scenes and a six-position song list play it back for you. At 90 BPM in 4/4 it lasts about 2 minutes 51 seconds.

You will make: a 64-bar old-school hip-hop beat with a sung phrase you sampled, six scenes and a six-position song list, in project 8.

Do one numbered step at a time, and stop playback before editing notes. Written for OS 2.5.1; older OS: update first. Finish 01 First beat and 02 Make it a song before you start. 07 Sample, chop & resample and 08 Play & perform explain sampling and live playing in more depth. The app cannot see your hardware: trust your display and your ears.

What you need

Your K.O. II, headphones or speakers plugged into its output, paper and a pencil for scene numbers, and one source for a short sung phrase:

• A phone or computer connected with a USB-C cable (OS 2.5 USB audio).
• A phone, laptop or turntable with a headphone or line-level output, connected to the K.O. II's 3.5 mm input with a stereo 3.5 mm cable.
• No cable at all: the built-in mic and your own voice.

Turntable tip: the official guide says to use an RIAA amplifier or a turntable with a built-in amp. If your turntable has a PHONO/LINE switch, as the Audio-Technica LP60X family does, set it to LINE. See Audio-Technica's LP60X setup guide.

01 / Start a fresh project and rebuild your beat

  1. Press MAIN; if anything plays, press PLAY to stop it. Hold MAIN, hold pad 8 as well, then release both. The official guide says “hold main and one of the pads (1-9) to select an empty project”. This course keeps project 6 for 01, 02 and 08, 7 for 07 and 9 for 10 (your scratch project from 00 Start here until then), so 8 is free. Press PLAY: nothing should sound. Stop, tap A, then tap pads 1, 2 and 3: silent too. Your 02 song stays untouched in project 6, with its pads and song list. If a pad makes a sound, you may still be in another project: on OS 2.0 and later a quick MAIN + number tap can select a scene instead, so hold pad 8 a moment longer and check again. Project 8 already holds work you want to keep? See help “My project is not empty” in Troubleshooting; the sounds you load next replace whatever a reused project’s pads hold. Music on this unit you care about? Back it up first with the official EP sample tool.

  2. Press SOUND, tap A, then tap pad 1. Hold SOUND, type 1 on the pads and release SOUND: A1 loads sample 001. Tap pad 2, hold SOUND, type 1, 0, 0 and release (sample 100). Tap pad 3, hold SOUND, type 2, 0, 0 and release (sample 200). These are the 01 First beat sounds: factory kicks are stored at 1–99, snares at 100–199 and hi-hats at 200–299. Tap pads 1, 2 and 3: a low kick, a sharp snare, a short bright tick. With pad 3 selected, turn X down until the hat is clearly quieter than the kick. A pad silent, or a long sound? Browse that range with − / + and keep a short one.

  3. Press TEMPO and turn X until the display shows 90 (or hold TEMPO and type 9, 0). Press TIMING and turn X until it shows 16 (not 16T). Press − to choose quantize. Leave Y (swing) alone. Press MAIN. At 90 BPM one bar lasts about 2.7 seconds.

  4. Stopped, in MAIN, with A selected: hold SHIFT and press − until the display shows 1.1.1. Hold RECORD, tap pad 1, release. Use + to reach 1.2.3, 1.3.1 and 1.4.3 and write pad 1 the same way at each. Pad 1 lights at all four positions: a lit pad means a note is written on that step.

  5. Still stopped in group A, write pad 3 (hold RECORD, tap pad 3) at 1.1.1, 1.1.3, 1.2.1, 1.2.3, 1.3.1, 1.3.3, 1.4.1 and 1.4.3: every .1 and .3. Some of these already hold a kick; a hat there too is correct. Press PLAY: kick and steady hats. Press PLAY to stop.

  6. Now play the snare live. Practise first: press PLAY and, without touching RECORD, tap pad 2 on beats two and four (count 1, TWO, 3, FOUR). Press PLAY to stop. For real: tap and release RECORD, then press PLAY. After the four-beat count-in, tap pad 2 on two and four, then press RECORD: recording stops and the beat keeps playing. Listen, then press PLAY to stop. Quantize snaps your taps to the 16-step grid.

  7. Checkpoint. Stopped, step to 1.2.1 and 1.4.1: pad 2 lights at both and nowhere else. Press PLAY.

    You should hear the 01 First beat drums: kick on 1, the “and” of 2, 3 and the “and” of 4; snare on 2 and 4; hats on every eighth note. Stop.

    If it doesn't: press SHIFT + B (undo) while the umbrella is lit and record the snare again. Or remove a stray note with a brief ERASE + its pad at its position, then write pad 2 at 1.2.1 and 1.4.1 with RECORD held, as in 01 First beat.

02 / Add the bass and get ready for the vocal

  1. Tap B. Press SOUND, tap pad 1, hold SOUND, type 4, 0, 0 and release: B1 loads sample 400 (factory bass sounds are stored at 400–499). Tap pad 1: one low bass note. Turn X down if it booms over the kick.

  2. Press MAIN. Stopped, with B selected, go to 1.1.1, hold RECORD, tap pad 1 and release. Do the same at 1.3.1. Pad 1 lights at both positions. Press PLAY: the bass joins the kick on beats 1 and 3. Stop.

  3. Tap C. Hold RECORD, press + once, then release both: the display shows C's pattern length change from 1 to 2. Tap D and do the same. A and B stay one bar and repeat under the two-bar C and D patterns, as in 02 Make it a song. C and D stay empty for now.

  4. Optional groove (OS 2.5 per-pad time shift): stopped, tap A, then press SHIFT + TIMING to open timing correct. Hold pad 2 and press + three times, then release and press MAIN. Each press moves every A2 snare one tick (1/96 of a beat) later. Press PLAY: the snare sits a hair behind the beat, a laid-back hip-hop feel. Stop. Too much? Open timing correct again and tap pad 2 once: a tap there snaps all its notes back to the grid.

  5. Hold MAIN and read the scene number: write it down as VERSE (drums and bass, no vocal). Release MAIN and press SHIFT + MAIN once. Hold MAIN again: the number has changed, because commit copied VERSE and moved you into the copy. Write the new number as HOOK. You build the vocal in HOOK; VERSE stays as it is.

  6. Checkpoint. In HOOK press PLAY. You should hear the drums plus two bass pulses per bar, exactly like VERSE. Stop. If it doesn't: no bass? Tap B and check that pad 1 lights at 1.1.1 and 1.3.1, and that pad 1 makes a sound in SOUND. The scene number did not change? Press SHIFT + MAIN once more and read the number again.

Official guide: pattern length · timing correct · commit

03 / Choose your source and the vocal phrase

  1. Turn your source's volume and your headphones down first, then set up ONE source. USB: connect a phone or computer with a USB-C cable and pick the K.O. II as that device's sound output (OS 2.5 USB audio). Line: run a stereo 3.5 mm cable from a phone, laptop or turntable output into the K.O. II's 3.5 mm input, not its output, MIDI or SYNC socket. Mic: connect nothing and use the built-in mic. Keep your headphones or speakers on the K.O. II output.

  2. Play your song and find TWO TO FOUR sung words you like, about one to two seconds long, ideally where the backing music is quiet. This is your PHRASE. Write down where it is, for example the time shown on your phone. Sampling a finished song records the backing music too; the K.O. II does not separate the voice. Using the mic? Choose two to four words you will sing or say.

  3. Pick one short word or syllable from inside the PHRASE as your ANSWER, ideally half a second or less, and write it next to PHRASE. You record the phrase only once: later you copy it to a second pad and trim that copy down to the ANSWER.

  4. Tap C, then press SAMPLE: the pads blink, ready to record. Do not press a pad yet. Press − / + to choose the input; the official list names them mic, in (line input) and usb, each with mono and stereo versions where offered. Check your display. Mono is fine for a voice and allows samples up to 40 seconds on OS 2.5. Play the song and turn X (input level) up until it is strong but not harsh. Turn Y (threshold) low so recording starts as soon as you hold a pad.

  5. Checkpoint. Play the song. With a line input you should hear it through the K.O. II as you turn X. If it doesn't: check the input on the display, that the cable is in the K.O. II's input, that the source volume is up and, on a turntable, that LINE is set. USB: make the K.O. II the phone's sound output. Still silent? System setting 511 (USB input: sampler + live monitoring) turns monitoring on: with the K.O. II already on, press SHIFT + ERASE, type 5, 1, 1, press ENTER, then MAIN (510 is the default). Never hold SHIFT + ERASE while switching the K.O. II on: that formats the drive, and the factory sounds cannot be recovered. Mic: you may only hear yourself on playback.

04 / Record the phrase once and trim it

  1. Still in SAMPLE with C selected, start the song a few seconds before the PHRASE. Just BEFORE the first word, hold pad 1 (C1) and keep holding through the words; release just AFTER the last word. C1 stops blinking and stays lit: it holds your sample. Press MAIN, tap C and tap pad 1: you hear your phrase. A little extra audio at either end is fine; you trim it next. Need both hands for the source? Hands-free sampling (OS 2.0 and later): hold SHIFT and press pad 1 just before the words, then press SAMPLE right after them. Not happy? Press SAMPLE and record pad 1 again by holding it.

  2. Press SOUND, tap C, then tap pad 1 to select C1. Press SHIFT + C once to copy its sound. Tap pad 2, then press SHIFT + D to paste. Tap pad 2: C2 plays the same phrase. Press SHIFT + C only once here; pressing it twice copies every sound in the group.

  3. Tap pad 1 to select C1. Briefly press SHIFT + SOUND to open sound edit, then press − / + until the display shows the trim page (TRI). Turn X (start) a little at a time, tapping pad 1 between changes, until playback starts just before the first word. Do not cut into the word. Hold SHIFT while turning for finer steps; on OS 2.5, holding the pad here shows the playback position in seconds. Trim changes how this pad plays in this project; the recording itself is untouched.

  4. Never hold SHIFT + SOUND on this page: holding it crops the recording on disk for good, and C2 (and later D1) play the same recording.

    Stay on the trim page. Turn Y (length) down, tapping pad 1, until playback stops just after the LAST word. Keep every word clear. At 90 BPM one bar lasts about 2.7 seconds, so keep the phrase shorter than that.

  5. Press SOUND, tap pad 2 (C2), briefly press SHIFT + SOUND and go to the trim page again. Turn X until playback starts at your ANSWER word, then turn Y until it stops right after it: about half a second or less. Tap pad 1, then pad 2: phrase, then only the answer word.

  6. Still editing C2, press − / + until you reach the page where X sets the play mode (oneshot, key or legato), and turn X to oneshot. Do the same for C1: press SOUND, tap pad 1, briefly press SHIFT + SOUND, find that page, turn X to oneshot. Oneshot plays the whole trimmed sound from one tap. Press MAIN, then SOUND: here X is the selected pad's volume. Set pads 1 and 2 so the words are clear but not louder than the snare. Leave Y (pitch) alone. Press MAIN.

  7. Checkpoint. In MAIN tap C, then pads 1 and 2. You should hear the whole phrase from its first word to its last, then only the ANSWER word. If it doesn't: a word is clipped? Back on the trim page, turn X down or Y up. The pad plays a different sound? You pressed − / + in SOUND instead of sound edit, which changes the pad's sample: press − / + back until it plays your phrase again. Harsh or crackly? Record pad 1 again with a lower X in SAMPLE, then copy it to pad 2 again.

05 / Put the phrase and answer into HOOK

  1. Hold MAIN and check the number is HOOK. Stopped, in MAIN, tap C. Hold SHIFT and press − until the display shows 1.1.1. Hold RECORD, tap pad 1, release. This writes the full phrase at the start of bar 1. Tapping a pad without RECORD only auditions it.

  2. Press PLAY, listen to one two-bar pass, then stop. The phrase should start right at the top of bar 1, with room after it. If the first word starts noticeably late, press SOUND, tap C, tap pad 1, briefly press SHIFT + SOUND, go to the trim page (TRI) and turn X a little at a time until playback starts right on the first word, then press MAIN. In plain SOUND, X is volume, not trim. Quantize puts the trigger on the grid; it does not retime the words inside the recording.

  3. Stopped, with C selected: press SHIFT + + once to jump to bar 2 (the display shows 2.1.1), then press + until it shows 2.3.1: bar 2, beat 3. Hold RECORD, tap pad 2, release. The ANSWER now lands in bar 2, after the phrase. SHIFT + − / + jumps bars only while RECORD is not armed and no pad is held.

  4. Step through C with − / +: only two notes light, pad 1 at 1.1.1 and pad 2 at 2.3.1. Count it: full phrase in bar 1, space, short answer in bar 2. If the phrase still runs into the answer, choose a shorter phrase rather than cutting a word in half.

  5. Checkpoint. Press PLAY for two full passes, then stop.

    You should hear the drums and bass under the phrase in bar 1 and the answer in bar 2.

    If it doesn't: no vocal? Check that you are in HOOK and that C was selected while you wrote. An extra note? Go to it and remove it with a brief ERASE + its pad. The sampled backing music keeps its own rhythm and will not follow 90 BPM; if it fights the drums, choose a phrase with less music behind it.

Official guide: step sequence · navigating bars

06 / Make the bass fit the voice

  1. Press PLAY and listen to the bass with the words. If it clashes, stop, press SOUND, tap B, tap pad 1 and turn Y (pitch) a little, then play again. Change the bass, not the singer: leave C1 and C2 at their original pitch. Pad settings belong to the whole project, so VERSE changes too.

  2. Nothing fits? In SOUND with B1 selected, browse other bass sounds in 400–499 with − / +. Still clashing? Drop the bass from HOOK only: stopped, hold MAIN and check the number is HOOK, tap B, hold ERASE + pad 1 until TRK blinks, then release.

    Cleared the wrong pad? Press SHIFT + B at once, while the umbrella is lit.

    Every B1 note in HOOK is gone; VERSE keeps its bass.

    Drums plus a good vocal can work without bass.

  3. Balance in SOUND, one pad at a time: tap A, tap pad 3 and lower X if the hats cover the words; tap C and set pads 1 and 2 so the words are easy to follow; tap B, tap pad 1 and keep it below the kick. Make one change, listen, stop. In MAIN, X sets the input level, not pad volume.

  4. Checkpoint. Play HOOK. You should hear the drums, the bass (unless you dropped it) and the phrase and answer, every word clear. Hold MAIN, select VERSE and play: drums and bass, no vocal. Stop. If it doesn't: VERSE lost its bass? You cleared it in the wrong scene: press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit, or write pad 1 again at 1.1.1 and 1.3.1 in VERSE's group B. Words buried? Lower the hats and the bass in SOUND rather than raising the vocal.

Official guide: sound mode · erase and undo

07 / Make the intro and the break

  1. Hold MAIN, select VERSE with − / +, release. Press SHIFT + MAIN, then hold MAIN and write the new number as INTRO. Stopped, tap B, hold ERASE + pad 1 until TRK blinks, then release: all of INTRO's bass notes are gone in one move.

    Cleared the wrong pad? Press SHIFT + B at once, while the umbrella is lit.

  2. Press PLAY: INTRO is drums only. Stop. Hold MAIN and select VERSE: its bass is still there. Select HOOK: its vocal is still there. Copies are independent. If an earlier scene changed, press SHIFT + B at once while the umbrella is lit.

  3. Hold MAIN, select INTRO again and release. Press SHIFT + MAIN once and write the new number as BREAK: your copy for the stripped-down section.

  4. Hold MAIN first: the number must be BREAK.

    In BREAK, stopped, tap A. Hold ERASE + pad 2 until TRK blinks, then release: every snare in BREAK is gone.

    Cleared the wrong pad? Press SHIFT + B at once, while the umbrella is lit.

  5. Still in group A, hold ERASE + pad 3 until TRK blinks to clear the hats.

    Cleared the wrong pad? Press SHIFT + B at once, while the umbrella is lit.

    Then go to 1.1.1 and write pad 3 with RECORD held, and again at 1.3.1: two hats per bar, on beats 1 and 3.

  6. Checkpoint. Play BREAK. You should hear your kick rhythm with two hats per bar, and no snare, bass or vocal. It should feel empty: that makes the return feel bigger. Stop. If it doesn't: hold MAIN and check the number is BREAK. If INTRO lost its snare or hats, you cleared them there: select INTRO and press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit, or write them again (snare at 1.2.1 and 1.4.1, hats on every .1 and .3).

Official guide: erase a pad's notes (TRK) · commit

08 / Add the twist and the ending

  1. Hold MAIN and select HOOK. Play it once to check, then stop. Press SHIFT + MAIN and write the new number as RETURN. Only RETURN gets the twist.

  2. Make the twist sound from the same recording: press SOUND, tap C, tap pad 1 and press SHIFT + C once. Tap D, tap pad 1 and press SHIFT + D. Tap pad 1: D1 plays your phrase.

  3. With D1 selected, briefly press SHIFT + SOUND and go to the trim page. Set X and Y so only one short word or syllable plays, about 0.2 seconds. On the play-mode page turn X to oneshot. Press MAIN, then SOUND, and lower D1's X so it is a small detail.

  4. Optional (OS 2.5 reverse): with D1 selected, briefly press SHIFT + SOUND and press − / + until the display shows tim. Turn X until it shows rev, then tap pad 1: the word plays backwards. If it plays a different part of the recording, check D1's trim. Prefer it forwards? Turn X back to where it was. Press MAIN.

  5. Hold MAIN and check the number is RETURN. Stopped, tap D. Go to 1.1.1, press SHIFT + + to jump to bar 2, then + until the display shows 2.4.1. Hold RECORD, tap pad 1, release. Press + twice to reach 2.4.3 and write pad 1 again. Play two passes: the word repeats twice near the end of bar 2.

  6. Hold MAIN, select INTRO, release. Press SHIFT + MAIN and write the new number as END. Stopped, tap A, hold ERASE + pad 1 until TRK blinks (all kicks gone), then go to 1.1.1 and write pad 1 with RECORD held.

    Cleared the wrong pad? Press SHIFT + B at once, while the umbrella is lit.

    END keeps the snare and hats, with one kick per bar.

  7. Checkpoint. Play RETURN: HOOK plus the short word twice near the end of bar 2. Play HOOK: no extra word. Play END: one kick per bar with snare and hats, no bass, no vocal. Stop.

    If it doesn't: the word also plays in HOOK? In HOOK, tap D and hold ERASE + pad 1 until TRK blinks. The two hits cut each other off? Shorten D1 a little more on the trim page, or remove the note at 2.4.3 with a brief ERASE + pad 1.

Official guide: copy sounds · time page and reverse · what's new in OS 2.5

09 / Set the section lengths and build the song

  1. Read the song map below and keep your six scene numbers beside you. A song position plays its scene once, for as long as the scene's longest pattern. A and B are one bar and repeat, so C decides how long each scene lasts. Doubling C makes a section longer and, in HOOK and RETURN, repeats the vocal too.

  2. Hold MAIN and select INTRO. Stopped, tap C. Tap RECORD to arm it, hold SHIFT and press + once, release SHIFT, then tap RECORD again to disarm. The official guide says this “will duplicate notes in the existing pattern and double the length”, so C goes from 2 to 4 bars: check the display. Do the same in END.

  3. Select BREAK. Tap C, arm RECORD, hold SHIFT and press + twice: 2 → 4 → 8 bars. Disarm RECORD and check the display.

  4. Do the same in VERSE, HOOK and RETURN, pressing + three times in each: 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 bars. In HOOK and RETURN the vocal notes are copied along, so the phrase still plays every two bars. Doubled the wrong group? Press SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

  5. Stopped, hold MAIN and press ENTER (the pad) to open the song list editor (OS 2.0 and later). This project's list starts with one position. Hold SHIFT and press − / + until position 1 holds INTRO's scene number.

  6. Hold SHIFT and press A to add a position; still holding SHIFT, use − / + to set its scene to VERSE. Add four more the same way: HOOK, BREAK, RETURN, END. Release SHIFT and step through with − / +: exactly six positions, in the song map's order. Add while you are on the last position. An extra position? Select it and press SHIFT + C to cut it.

  7. Checkpoint. Press SHIFT + PLAY to play the song from the start, and listen without touching anything. You should hear INTRO, VERSE, HOOK, BREAK, RETURN and END, about 2 minutes 51 seconds, and then it stops by itself. If it doesn't: much shorter? A scene's C is still two bars, or a position is missing. Much longer? A pattern is longer than planned: check C's length in that scene. It keeps looping? Song loop is on: system setting 420 turns it off.

10 / Finish, perform and keep your work

  1. In the song list (hold MAIN, press ENTER), press SHIFT + PLAY to play the song from the beginning at a comfortable volume. Listen for kick and snare first, then the bass, then whether the words are easy to understand. Stop and change only one pad volume in SOUND if needed. Pad settings affect every scene, so listen through again.

  2. Optional space: in the song list press SHIFT + PLAY to start the song, then tap C, press FX and choose reverb with − / +, then raise the fader a little: that is C's reverb send. Keep B's send low. Press MAIN when done. Do not hold RECORD while moving the fader: that records fader automation. Recorded some by accident? Stopped, hold ERASE + FADER until FDR blinks to clear the group's fader moves (on OS 2.5, ERASE + FADER + a pad clears only that pad's fader function).

  3. Listen to BREAK going into RETURN: open the song list (hold MAIN, press ENTER), select position 4 with − / + and press PLAY; the song plays on from there.

    The bare break should make the phrase, answer and repeated word feel bigger.

    If the repeated word distracts, stop, press MAIN, hold MAIN to select RETURN, tap D and hold ERASE + pad 1 until TRK blinks. Change one thing at a time.

  4. Optional, live: perform the song by hand. With the K.O. II already on, press SHIFT + ERASE (system settings), type 4, 1, 1, press ENTER, then MAIN: a scene you pick now starts at the end of the current bar (412 would wait for the end of the pattern instead). Press MAIN to leave the song list, hold MAIN to select INTRO, and press PLAY. In the last bar of each section, hold MAIN and press + or − to pick the next scene, following the song map's bar counts. Hold FX and press pads for punch-in effects; 08 Play & perform has more. Afterwards set 410 the same way to switch scenes instantly again.

  5. Record your song. On OS 2.5, connect the K.O. II to a computer or phone with USB-C and choose the K.O. II as the recording app's input (USB audio out), or plug the K.O. II's output into a recorder or audio interface. Start recording there, then press SHIFT + PLAY in the song list. Commit copies scenes; it is not an audio export, and this coach cannot record your K.O. II.

  6. Write the project number and the INTRO, VERSE, HOOK, BREAK, RETURN and END scene numbers on paper. Back up the project with the official EP sample tool on a computer. Your ticks are saved only in this browser. For public release of music containing someone else's record, check and obtain the necessary permissions; do not assume sampling automatically grants rights.

  7. Checkpoint. In the song list, play the song once more with SHIFT + PLAY. You should hear a finished beat of about 2:51: a short drum intro, the groove, the hook with your phrase and answer, a bare break, the return with the twist and a short ending. If it doesn't: find the one scene that sounds wrong, hold MAIN to select it, fix it there, then play the song again.
