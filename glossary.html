Tools

Glossary

106 words used in this course and the official guide, explained.

#ABCDEFGHIKLMNOPQRSTUV

0–9

4/4
The time signature this course uses: four beats per bar.

A

Amplitude (AMP)
The volume of one pad’s sound. X in SOUND mode.
Arpeggio
A chord played one note after another. In KEYS, hold TIMING and press several pads: they play in the order you pressed them (OS 2.5). The sound’s play mode must be oneshot or legato.
Attack chop
Auto chop mode that slices at the loudest parts of a sample; suits drums and other percussive sounds.
Audition
Tapping a pad just to hear it. Without RECORD held, nothing is written.
Automation
Fader movement recorded into a pattern by holding RECORD while moving the fader, so it replays every loop. Holding ERASE + FADER until FDR blinks erases it.

B

Backbeat
Snare on beats 2 and 4, as in this course’s first beat.
Backup / restore
Saving a copy of your K.O. II’s projects and sounds to a computer with the official EP sample tool, and putting it back later. Commit is not a backup.
Bar
A group of beats. In 4/4, one bar has four beats.
Beat
The steady pulse you would tap your foot to. Count “one, two, three, four”.
BPM
Beats per minute: the tempo. At 90 BPM one bar lasts about 2.7 seconds.

C

Chop
Cutting one sample into slices, each on its own pad. SHIFT + SAMPLE opens chop: auto chop (equal length or attack) or live chop. Choosing the group for an auto chop resets that group’s pads.
Class compliant
Works without a special driver: the computer or phone treats the K.O. II as a standard USB audio device.
Commit
SHIFT + MAIN: saves the current scene and moves you into a new copy of it. Not a backup file.
Count-in
Four metronome beats before live recording starts: tap RECORD, then press PLAY.
Crop
Permanently cutting the sample file to its trim points (hold SHIFT + SOUND on the trim page). Cannot be undone.

D

DEL / CLR
Display messages when you hold ERASE + MAIN for two seconds: DEL means an empty scene that was not in the song was deleted; CLR means the scene’s contents were cleared.
Disk low
A display warning when you press SAMPLE and there is not enough space left to record 20 seconds.
Drop / build
In dance music, the drop is the full-energy part; the build is the rising part just before it.
Ducking
Turning a sound down briefly so another one stands out: what sidechain does.

E

Eighth notes
Two even notes per beat: count “1 and 2 and…”. With 16 steps per bar they sit on every .1 and .3.
Envelope (attack, release)
How a sound fades in (attack) and how it fades out after you let go (release).
EP sample tool
teenage engineering’s browser tool for moving samples between the K.O. II and a computer over USB-C. It also backs up and restores your work.
EQU (equal-length chop)
Auto chop mode that cuts a sample into even slices, good for loops. In chop, hold the group pad and choose EQU with − / +, release it, then choose the number of slices with − / + (OS 2.5).

F

Factory sounds
The samples a new K.O. II comes with, stored by number: kicks 1–99, snares 100–199, hi-hats 200–299, percussion 300–399, bass 400–499, melodic sounds 500–599. Formatting the drive erases them for good.
Fader
The slider. By default it sets the selected group’s level; in FX it sets that group’s send.
Firmware (OS)
The K.O. II’s own software version. This course is written for OS 2.5.1; some features need a minimum OS, so update an older unit first.
Format (FMT)
Erasing the whole drive by holding SHIFT + ERASE while switching on; FMT shows for about 10 seconds. It removes all your work and all factory sounds, which cannot be recovered. Not a casual reset.
Four-on-the-floor
A kick on every beat (1.1.1, 1.2.1, 1.3.1, 1.4.1), typical of house music.
FX / master effect
The one shared effect (delay, reverb, distortion, chorus, filter or compressor) that every group can send to.
FX send
How much of a group goes into the shared effect. Set with the fader in FX mode.

G

Groove
The main repeating feel of a track, usually drums plus bass.
Group
One of four banks (A, B, C, D), each with 12 pads and its own patterns. Tap a group button to select it.

H

Hands-free sampling
In SAMPLE, SHIFT + a pad keeps recording without holding the pad; press SAMPLE to stop.
Hook
The short, catchy part listeners remember, often a vocal phrase.

I

Input level / gain
How loud incoming audio is when sampling. X in SAMPLE mode.
Input source
What SAMPLE records from, chosen with − / +: mic, line in (mono, stereo, left or right), resample (rsp, mono or stereo) or usb (mono or stereo).
Intro / breakdown / outro
Song sections: the opening, a stripped-back part that makes the next part hit harder, and the ending.

K

Key mode (play mode)
Play mode: polyphonic, so the same sample can sound several times at once. Not the same as KEYS mode; arpeggio needs oneshot or legato instead.
KEYS
A mode that plays one sample at 12 different pitches across the pads.

L

Latch
Keeping something going after you let go. Note repeat latch: press TIMING, then hold SHIFT and press a pad; do the same again to stop it.
Layer
One part of a beat, such as the kick, hats or bass, added on top of the others.
Legato
Play mode: monophonic, plays a sample one at a time. Arpeggio works with legato or oneshot sounds.
Line input
The 3.5 mm input for sampling a phone or synth instead of the built-in mic.
Live chop
Chopping by hand: in chop mode, press pads while the sample plays to place the cuts.
Live monitoring
Hearing incoming audio through the K.O. II as it arrives. For USB input, system setting 511 turns it on; 510, the default, sends USB audio to the sampler only.
Live recording
Playing pads while the loop runs and RECORD is on.
Lock mode (LOK)
Holding MAIN while switching on: LOK shows, and changes to projects, patterns, scenes, pads and settings are not kept after a restart.
Loop
A pattern repeating over and over.
Loop function
SHIFT + TEMPO repeats a short part of the pattern as a performance effect. X slides the loop, Y sets its length; TEMPO exits at once, MAIN on the next bar.

M

Metronome
The click that keeps time while you record. TEMPO Y sets its volume.
Mixing
Setting how loud each part is compared with the others.
Mono / stereo
Mono is one audio channel, stereo two (left and right). Since OS 2.5, mono samples can be up to 40 seconds long.
Mute group
A sound-edit setting that groups samples you do not want playing at the same time, such as an open and a closed hi-hat.

N

Note interval (16, 8, 16T)
TIMING X: how many steps a bar has. 16 means 16 steps per bar. T means triplet: 16T has 24 steps per bar.
Note repeat
Hold TIMING and press a pad: the pad repeats at the current note interval.

O

Off-beat
A note between the main beats, such as the “and” after a beat (1.1.3).
Oneshot
Play mode: monophonic, plays the whole sample, one at a time. Arpeggio works with oneshot or legato sounds.
Output / master compressor
SHIFT + FX: the compressor on the whole mix. X sets drive, Y speed.
Overdub
Recording new notes on top of a pattern that already has notes.

P

Pad
One of the 12 buttons that play sounds. In many modes they also work as a number pad.
Pan
Where a sound sits between left and right. Y on the sound page of sound edit.
Pattern
The notes one group plays and when. Each group has up to 99 patterns, each up to 99 bars long.
Pattern length
How many bars a pattern lasts before it repeats. New patterns are one bar.
Phrase
A short musical idea, like a sentence in music: a few notes or words that belong together.
Pickup
A note just before a main beat that leads into it, like the kicks at 1.2.3 and 1.4.3.
Pitch (PTC)
How high or low a sound plays. Y in SOUND mode.
Play mode (oneshot / key / legato)
How a pad plays its sample; X on the sound page of sound edit. The official guide: oneshot is monophonic and plays the whole sample, one at a time; key is polyphonic, so several copies of the same sample can sound at once; legato is monophonic and plays a sample one at a time.
Position (1.2.3)
What the display shows in MAIN: bar . beat . step within the beat. 1.2.3 is bar 1, beat 2, third step (grid step 7).
Project
One whole composition. The K.O. II holds nine projects; on a new unit 1–5 hold factory demos and 6–9 are empty.
Punch-in FX
Temporary effects played by holding FX and pressing pads while the beat runs.

Q

Quantize
Snapping what you record live to the nearest step. Free time records exactly as you play.

R

Record arm
RECORD pressed and waiting: the next PLAY starts recording.
Resample (RSP)
Sampling the K.O. II’s own output, for example a whole pattern or a stack of effects, into one new sample. In SAMPLE, press + until rsp shows.
Resample bars
With hands-free sampling engaged, − / + sets how many bars to record; pressing PLAY starts it. Without a bar count, PLAY records the length of the selected pattern.
Reverb
An effect that adds the sound of a room. X sets its length, Y its colour.
Reverse (REV)
Playing a sample backwards: SHIFT + SOUND, press + until TIM, then turn X to REV (OS 2.5 and later).

S

Sample
A short audio recording used as an instrument. The K.O. II stores up to 999.
Sample rate
How many times per second audio is measured. The K.O. II records at 46,875 Hz by default (system setting 502); 500 (26,250 Hz) and 501 (32,000 Hz) record smaller, more lo-fi samples.
Save sound edits
Holding SHIFT + SOUND for two seconds saves your edits to the current sound. On the trim page the same hold crops the file instead.
Scale / key / octave
In KEYS: which notes the pads offer (scale: hold KEYS, turn Y), which note they start from (key: hold KEYS, turn X) and how high they play (octave: hold KEYS, press − / +). OS 2.5 added blues, harmonic minor and melodic minor and saves key and scale per project.
Scene
A saved combination of the four groups’ patterns: one section of a song, such as INTRO or BREAK.
Sidechain
Briefly turning chosen groups down each time a source sound, such as the kick, plays. SHIFT + FX opens output and + switches to sidechain. Hold a group pad and select a sound as the source; hold SHIFT and press a group pad to add a destination. X sets how long the duck lasts, Y its shape.
Slice
One piece of a chopped sample, played from its own pad.
Solo
Hearing one group alone. Holding FX and pressing a group button solos it while playing.
Song list / song position
In song mode, the numbered positions (up to 99) that play scenes in order. Each position plays its scene once.
Song mode
Automatic playback of the song list. Hold MAIN and press ENTER to open its editor.
Sound edit
SHIFT + SOUND: pages for play mode, trim, envelope, time and more for the selected pad.
Stab
A short, punchy chord or sound hit.
Step
One slot in the sequencer grid. With TIMING at 16, a bar has 16 steps, four per beat.
Step entry
Placing notes one at a time while stopped: go to a position, hold RECORD and tap a pad.
Subdivision
A smaller slice of a beat. In 1.2.3 the last number is the subdivision.
Swing
Delays every second step slightly for a looser, bouncier feel. Works with the 8 and 16 intervals.
System settings
SHIFT + ERASE: device-wide options. Type a code such as 411 and press ENTER to jump to a setting; SHIFT + ENTER goes back a page and MAIN returns to your beat.

T

Tail
The part of a sound that keeps ringing after the hit. Long tails can blur a beat.
Tempo match
Hold SAMPLE + TEMPO and play audio into the mic or line in: the K.O. II finds its tempo and sets the project tempo to match.
Threshold
In SAMPLE, how loud a sound must be before recording starts. Y in SAMPLE mode.
Time shift / nudge
Moving notes slightly earlier or later than the grid. Hold SHIFT and a pad, then press − / + to nudge that note; to move all notes on a pad, use timing correct (SHIFT + TIMING). The official guide calls this offset notes.
Time stretch (BPM / BAR)
Time page (TIM) of sound edit. BPM stretches a sample to the project tempo once you set the sample’s own BPM with Y; BAR fits it to a chosen number of bars.
Timing correct
SHIFT + TIMING: snaps notes you already recorded on a pad to the grid.
Trim
Choosing where a sample starts and how long it plays (sound edit, trim page: X start, Y length). Reversible: the file is unchanged until you crop.
TRK / PTN / SND / FDR
Display messages while erasing: TRK when ERASE + a pad (stopped) clears every note of that pad in the pattern; PTN when ERASE + a group clears its pattern; SND when ERASE + SOUND deletes the sample; FDR when ERASE + FADER erases fader automation.

U

Undo (umbrella)
SHIFT + B undoes the last change. The umbrella icon lights when undo is possible.
Update utility
teenage engineering’s browser tool for installing the latest OS on the K.O. II (teenage.engineering/apps/update).
USB audio
Sound over the USB-C cable to and from a class-compliant computer or phone (OS 2.5 and later). The K.O. II can sample from USB (source usb), and the other device can record it or play through it.

V

Velocity
How hard a pad is hit. Pad velocity is off by default; system settings 301 and 302 turn it on (for soft or hard playing), 300 turns it off.
