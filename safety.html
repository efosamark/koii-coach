Safety and erasing

Every action in this course that erases or overwrites something on the K.O. II, how to avoid doing it by accident, and how to undo it where you can.

Undo

Press SHIFT + B while the umbrella icon is lit. The umbrella means undo is possible; once it is off, undo cannot bring the change back. The guide doesn’t say how many changes undo goes back; owners report only one (“there is one stage of undo”), so undo before you change anything else. Work in a copy of a scene (SHIFT + MAIN) when you try something, and back up with the official EP sample tool before anything that cannot be undone.

Official guide: Play and record (erase and undo)

Can be undone

Erase one note: a brief ERASE + pad

What it does: In stopped MAIN, at the note’s position, a brief ERASE + pad removes only the note on the current step.

Avoid it by accident: Keep the press short: holding ERASE + a pad until TRK blinks clears all of that pad’s notes.

Undo: SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

Official guide: Play and record

Clear a pad’s notes: hold ERASE + pad until TRK blinks

What it does: Clears every note of that pad in this pattern in one move.

Avoid it by accident: Hold MAIN first and check the scene number before erasing anything: the scene you mean to change must be the one selected.

Undo: SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

Official guide: Play and record

Clear a group’s pattern: hold ERASE + group until PTN blinks

What it does: The official guide says this erases “the current patterns”.

Avoid it by accident: Check that you are in the project and scene you mean, and back it up first.

Undo: SHIFT + B at once while the umbrella is lit, or restore your backup.

Official guide: Play and record (erase pattern)

No undo

Clear or delete a scene: hold ERASE + MAIN for 2 seconds

What it does: On a scene with notes, CLR shows: the scene is cleared, not deleted. Holding ERASE + MAIN for 2 seconds again on the empty scene deletes it (DEL blinks) when it is not in the song list.

Avoid it by accident: Only ever do this on a scene whose number you checked a moment ago, for example a copy made with SHIFT + MAIN.

No undo. This course does not rely on undo for a cleared scene.

Official guide: Play and record (erase scene)

Delete a sample: hold ERASE + SOUND until SND blinks

What it does: Deletes the selected sample from the K.O. II permanently.

Avoid it by accident: Check the sample’s number first. Deleting a sample is never needed to fix a misplaced hit: use undo (SHIFT + B) or a brief ERASE + pad.

No undo. A backup made with the official EP sample tool can bring the sample back.

Official guide: Play and record

Crop a sample: hold SHIFT + SOUND on the trim page

What it does: Overwrites the sample file with the trimmed sound.

Avoid it by accident: Pressing SHIFT + SOUND opens sound edit, and trimming there (X start, Y length) changes how the sound plays without changing the file. Never hold SHIFT + SOUND on the trim page unless you mean to crop.

No undo. The stored sample file is changed.

Official guide: Modes (sound edit)

At power-on

Format the drive: hold SHIFT + ERASE while switching on

What it does: Formats the drive: it removes all your work and all factory sounds, and the official guide says factory sounds cannot be recovered. It is not a quick reset.

Avoid it by accident: Switch the K.O. II on with your hands off the buttons. Before ever doing it, back up your projects with the official EP sample tool.

No undo. Only your own backup brings your work back.

Official guide: Erase drive

Lock mode: hold MAIN while switching on

What it does: LOK shows for about a second. Any changes you make are reset when you restart.

Avoid it by accident: Switch on with your hands off the buttons. Switch off and on again, holding nothing, before you build anything you want to keep, and check that LOK does not appear.

Undo: Restart without holding anything. Changes made in lock mode are not kept.

Official guide: System (lock mode)

Safe

System settings: SHIFT + ERASE while the K.O. II is on

Pressing SHIFT + ERASE while the K.O. II is already on only opens system settings. None of the codes in the settings table erases music.

Official guide: System
