Safety and erasing

Every action that erases or overwrites something on the K.O. II, how to avoid it, and how to undo it where you can.

Undo

Press SHIFT + B while the umbrella icon is lit. Once it is off, the change cannot be undone. Owners report one level of undo (Elektronauts), so undo before you change anything else. Try ideas in a copy of the scene (SHIFT + MAIN). Back up with the EP sample tool before anything that cannot be undone.

Official guide: Play and record (erase and undo)

Can be undone

Erase one note: a brief ERASE + pad

What it does: In stopped MAIN, at the note’s position, a brief ERASE + pad removes only that note.

Avoid it by accident: Keep the press short: holding ERASE + a pad until TRK blinks clears all of that pad’s notes.

Undo: SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

Official guide: Play and record

Clear a pad’s notes: hold ERASE + pad until TRK blinks

What it does: Clears all of that pad’s notes in this pattern.

Avoid it by accident: Hold MAIN and check the scene number first. Erase only in the scene you mean to change.

Undo: SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

Official guide: Play and record

Clear a group’s pattern: hold ERASE + group until PTN blinks

What it does: Erases the current patterns.

Avoid it by accident: Check you are in the project and scene you mean, and back it up first.

Undo: SHIFT + B at once while the umbrella is lit, or restore your backup.

Official guide: Play and record (erase pattern)

Erase a pad’s fader moves: stopped, hold ERASE + FADER and press the pad

What it does: Removes the fader moves recorded for that pad in this pattern. The notes stay.

Avoid it by accident: Hold MAIN and check the scene number first. Keep the press short: holding ERASE + FADER for 2 seconds, until FDR blinks, erases every fader move in the group’s pattern.

Undo: SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit. Otherwise record the move again.

Official guide: Play and record (fader)

No undo

Clear or delete a scene: hold ERASE + MAIN for 2 seconds

What it does: On a scene with notes, CLR shows and the scene is cleared. Hold ERASE + MAIN for 2 seconds again on the empty scene to delete it (DEL blinks), if it is not in the song list.

Avoid it by accident: Only do this on a scene whose number you checked a moment ago, such as a copy made with SHIFT + MAIN.

No undo. Treat a cleared scene as gone.

Official guide: Play and record (erase scene)

Delete a sample: hold ERASE + SOUND until SND blinks

What it does: Deletes the selected sample from the K.O. II permanently.

Avoid it by accident: Check the sample’s number first. To fix a misplaced hit, use undo (SHIFT + B) or a brief ERASE + pad instead.

No undo. Only a backup made with the EP sample tool can bring the sample back.

Official guide: Play and record

Crop a sample: hold SHIFT + SOUND on the trim page

What it does: Overwrites the sample file with the trimmed sound.

Avoid it by accident: SHIFT + SOUND opens sound edit. Trimming there with X (start) and Y (length) changes how the sound plays, not the file. Never hold SHIFT + SOUND on the trim page unless you mean to crop.

No undo. The stored sample file is changed.

Official guide: Modes (sound edit)

At power-on

Format the drive: hold SHIFT + ERASE while switching on

What it does: Formats the drive, removing all your work and all factory sounds. Factory sounds cannot be recovered. It is not a quick reset.

Avoid it by accident: Switch the K.O. II on with your hands off the buttons. Back up your projects with the EP sample tool before you ever format.

No undo. Only your own backup brings your work back.

Official guide: Erase drive

Lock mode: hold MAIN while switching on

What it does: LOK shows for about a second. Any changes you make are reset when you restart.

Avoid it by accident: Switch on with your hands off the buttons. Before building anything you want to keep, restart holding nothing and check that LOK does not appear.

Undo: Restart without holding anything. Changes made in lock mode are not kept.

Official guide: System (lock mode)

Safe

System settings: SHIFT + ERASE while the K.O. II is on

Pressing SHIFT + ERASE while the K.O. II is already on only opens system settings. None of the codes in the settings table erases music.
