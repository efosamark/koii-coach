01 Samples, notes and patterns

Tried

Understand

A sample is a sound. A pattern says when each sound plays. A new sample is not in your beat until you place it.

Try on the device

Record a sound in SAMPLE. Then, stopped in MAIN, go to a step, hold RECORD and tap the pad.

Official guide: Functions

02 Groups and scenes

Tried

Understand

Four groups, A to D, each have 12 pads and their own patterns. A scene is the patterns that play together: one section of a song. Good roles: A drums, B bass, C melody, D your own samples.

Try on the device

Tap a group to select it. Hold a group and press − / + to change its pattern. Hold MAIN and press − / + to change scene. SHIFT + MAIN commits: it saves the scene and moves you into a copy.

Official guide: Modes

03 Choosing and balancing sounds

Tried

Understand

SOUND chooses a pad’s sample. Here X sets its volume and Y its pitch. Factory sounds by type: kicks 1–99, snares 100–199, hats 200–299, percussion 300–399, bass 400–499, melodic 500–599.

Try on the device

Select a group, tap a pad, then browse with − / +. SHIFT + − / + jumps by ten. To jump to a number, hold SOUND and type it. Listen before you record.

Official guide: Modes

04 Live recording without panic

Tried

Understand

Stopped, tap RECORD, then press PLAY: four clicks count you in, then recording starts. Quantize (TIMING, then −) snaps what you play to the nearest step.

Try on the device

Play your pads after the count-in. RECORD stops recording and the beat keeps playing. PLAY stops both. SHIFT + B undoes a mistake while the umbrella icon is lit. Practise in a copy of a scene.

Official guide: Play and record

05 Precise step recording

Tried

Understand

Stopped in MAIN, − / + moves one step and SHIFT + − / + a whole bar. Hold RECORD and tap a pad to write a note on that step. The display shows bar.beat.step: 1.2.3 is bar 1, beat 2, third step.

Try on the device

Chapter 1’s placement helper shows where each position sits on the grid. Positions are not sample numbers.

Official guide: Play and record

06 Timing, swing and correction

Tried

Understand

In TIMING, X sets the grid: 16 means 16 steps per bar. Y adds swing. − snaps what you record to the grid, + keeps it as played. These settings change your next recording, not notes already there.

Try on the device

To snap notes already recorded, press SHIFT + TIMING and set the grid with X. Then, stopped, press a pad: all its notes snap to the grid.

Official guide: Functions

07 Longer loops

Tried

Understand

New patterns are one bar long, and each group has its own length. Added bars start empty.

Try on the device

Stopped, select the group, hold RECORD and press + to add a bar. − removes one. To double the length and copy the notes, press RECORD to arm it, then hold SHIFT and press +.

Official guide: Play and record

08 KEYS: one sample, many pitches

Tried

Understand

KEYS turns the pads into a 12-note keyboard that plays one sample at different pitches. Hold KEYS and press − / + to change octave.

Try on the device

Select a pad with a sound, press KEYS and play along with your drums. Hold KEYS and turn X to choose the key, Y the scale. For an arpeggio, hold TIMING and press several pads. Chapter 4 practises all of this.

Official guide: Play and record

09 Record your own sound

Tried

Understand

In SAMPLE, − / + chooses the input: mic, line in and more. X sets the input level. Y sets the threshold: how loud a sound must be to start recording.

Try on the device

Hold an unused pad, make a short sound, then let go. Tap the pad to hear it, then place it in your beat from MAIN. Nothing recorded? Check the source and threshold. Chapter 3 goes further.

Official guide: Functions

10 Trim and envelope safely

Tried

Understand

SHIFT + SOUND opens sound edit, and − / + turns the page. Trim: X start, Y length. Envelope: X fade-in, Y fade-out. Trimming never changes the sample file.

Try on the device

Make small edits and listen. Careful: on the trim page, holding SHIFT + SOUND crops the file for good. To save your edits to the sound, hold SHIFT + SOUND for two seconds away from the trim page. Holding ERASE + SOUND deletes the sample from the device.

Official guide: Modes

11 Effects and fader

Tried

Understand

All groups share one effect. In FX, − / + picks it and the fader sets how much of the selected group goes in. X and Y shape it.

Try on the device

Choose reverb and raise the fader: X sets its length, Y its colour. While the beat plays, hold FX and press pads for punch-in effects. Hold FX and a group button to solo it. Hold RECORD while moving the fader to record the move.

Official guide: Effects

12 Automatic song arrangement (song mode)

Tried

Understand

The song list plays your scenes in order. Each position plays its scene once, for as long as its longest pattern. Repeat a position to make a section longer.

Try on the device

Hold MAIN and press ENTER to open the list. SHIFT + A adds a position, and SHIFT + − / + picks its scene. − / + moves between positions, and SHIFT + C removes one. SHIFT + PLAY plays from the start.

Official guide: Modes

13 Undo and careful erasing

Tried

Understand

SHIFT + B is undo while the umbrella icon is lit. Erasing notes is not the same as deleting a sample.

Try on the device

Stopped in MAIN, a quick ERASE + pad removes that pad’s note on this step. Hold ERASE + pad until TRK blinks to clear all its notes in the pattern. Hold ERASE + MAIN for two seconds to erase the whole scene. Holding ERASE + SOUND until SND blinks deletes the sample from the device. Practise on a copy.

Official guide: Play and record

14 What OS 2.5.1 gives you, and where to learn it

Tried

Understand

Written for OS 2.5.1. OS 2.0 added song mode, resampling, hands-free sampling and sidechain. OS 2.5 added USB audio, reverse, equal-length chop, arpeggio and three more scales. OS 2.5.1 fixes USB audio from Android phones and a crash when cropping long samples. On an older OS, update first with Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II.

Try on the device

Try reverse: select a pad with a sound, press SHIFT + SOUND, press + until TIM shows and turn X until REV shows. Tap the pad: it plays backwards. Turn X back afterwards.

15 Copy and paste a bar

Tried

Understand

In MAIN, SHIFT + C copies: press once for the current bar, twice for the whole pattern. SHIFT + D pastes. Handy when a rhythm repeats.

Try on the device

Stopped in MAIN, select the group and go to the bar to copy. SHIFT + − / + jumps a bar. Press SHIFT + C once, move to a bar the pattern already has and press SHIFT + D. Press PLAY to listen. SHIFT + B undoes it while the umbrella is lit.

Official guide: Play and record

16 Projects, backup and starting fresh

Tried

Understand

A project is one whole song, and the K.O. II holds nine. On a new unit, 1–5 hold factory demos and 6–9 are empty. Commit copies a scene inside a project. It is not a backup.

Try on the device

To change project, hold MAIN, then hold a pad from 1–9 too while you watch the display. A quick press can select a scene instead, so check: in an empty project the pads make no sound. Back up with the EP sample tool on a computer, over USB-C. There is no harmless reset. Holding SHIFT + ERASE while switching on formats the drive, and factory sounds cannot be recovered.

Official guide: Workflow

17 Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II: update, back up, stay safe

Tried

Understand

Chapter 0 gets a new K.O. II ready: update it to OS 2.5.1, back it up, and learn which buttons held at power-on erase or lock it.

Try on the device

Never hold SHIFT + ERASE while switching on. It formats the drive, and factory sounds cannot be recovered. Holding MAIN while switching on starts lock mode: LOK shows, and changes are lost at the next restart. Updates come from teenage engineering’s update utility, in a web browser.
