19 lessons.

01 Samples, notes and patterns

Tried

Understand

A sample is audio; a pattern is the instructions for when to play it. Sampling your voice does not automatically place it in a beat.

Try on the device

Press SAMPLE to capture a sound, then MAIN to sequence it: stopped, navigate to a position, hold RECORD and tap the pad.

Official guide: Functions

02 Groups and scenes

Tried

Understand

A, B, C and D are four groups of 12 pads, each with its own patterns. A scene is the combination of patterns that play together. Suggested roles: A drums, B bass, C melody, D your own samples.

Try on the device

Tap a group to select it. Hold a group with − / + only when you want to change its pattern. Hold MAIN with − / + to choose a scene; holding MAIN and typing its number on the pads also works. SHIFT + MAIN commits: it saves the scene and moves you into a new copy. Watch the display while MAIN is held: holding MAIN with one of pads 1–9 is also how the official guide changes project.

Official guide: Modes

03 Choosing and balancing sounds

Tried

Understand

SOUND is for choosing a pad’s sample. In this mode X is amplitude (volume) and Y pitch. Factory categories: kick 1–99, snare 100–199, hat 200–299, percussion 300–399, bass 400–499, melodic 500–599.

Try on the device

Select a group and pad, then use − / + (SHIFT + − / + jumps by ten), or hold SOUND and type the number on the pads. Listen to a sound before recording it. Custom libraries may differ.

Official guide: Modes

04 Live recording without panic

Tried

Understand

While stopped, tap and release RECORD, then press PLAY for a four-beat count-in. RECORD + PLAY together starts without it. Quantize (TIMING, −) snaps what you play to the grid.

Try on the device

Play your pads after the count-in. Press RECORD to stop recording while playback continues, or PLAY to stop both. SHIFT + B undoes the last change while the umbrella is lit. Practise in a scene copy first.

Official guide: Play and record

05 Precise step recording

Tried

Understand

In stopped MAIN, − / + moves between positions and SHIFT + − / + moves a whole bar. Hold RECORD and tap a pad to write a note on that step; the pad then lights there. With TIMING at 16 in 4/4, 1.2.3 is grid step 7.

Try on the device

Use the First beat placement helper to see how grid steps match bar.beat.step positions. Do not confuse positions with sample numbers.

Official guide: Play and record

06 Timing, swing and correction

Tried

Understand

TIMING X sets the note interval (16 = 16 steps per bar); Y sets swing for the 8 and 16 intervals. − chooses quantized recording, + free time. These settings apply when you record, not to notes already written.

Try on the device

For notes already recorded, press SHIFT + TIMING (timing correct), set the interval with X, then, while stopped, press a pad to quantize all of its notes.

Official guide: Functions

07 Longer loops

Tried

Understand

New patterns begin at one bar, and each group has its own length. Extending a pattern adds empty bars; it does not copy notes.

Try on the device

Stopped, select the group, hold RECORD and press + to add a bar (− removes one). To double the length and copy the notes, press RECORD to arm, then hold SHIFT and press +. Check the selected group first.

Official guide: Play and record

08 KEYS: one sample, many pitches

Tried

Understand

Select a sample pad and press KEYS to play that sound across a 12-note keyboard on the pads. Hold KEYS with − / + to choose the octave.

Try on the device

Practise over your drums, stop, then tap RECORD and press PLAY to record with a count-in. Hold KEYS and turn X for the key and Y for the scale; OS 2.5 added blues, harmonic minor and melodic minor and saves key and scale per project. Arpeggio: in KEYS, hold TIMING and press several pads (oneshot or legato sounds only). Chapter 4 practises all of this.

Official guide: Play and record

09 Record your own sound

Tried

Understand

In SAMPLE choose the input (mic, line in and more) with − / +. X is input level and Y threshold. Hold an unused pad, make a short sound, then release.

Try on the device

Tap the pad to audition. Return to MAIN to place it in your beat. If capture never starts, check the source and threshold. To sample a computer or phone, connect it with a USB-C cable and press + until usb shows. Chapter 3 covers hands-free sampling, chop, reverse and resampling.

Official guide: Functions

10 Trim and envelope safely

Tried

Understand

SHIFT + SOUND opens sound edit; − / + chooses a page. On trim, X is start and Y length. On envelope, X is attack and Y release. Trimming does not change the stored sample file.

Try on the device

Audition small edits. Holding SHIFT + SOUND on the trim page crops the file permanently; ordinary trimming does not. The official guide also says holding SHIFT + SOUND for two seconds saves your edits to the current sound, so do that away from the trim page. Holding ERASE + SOUND deletes the sample from the device.

Official guide: Modes

11 Effects and fader

Tried

Understand

Groups send to one shared master effect. In FX, − / + selects it and the fader sets the selected group’s send; X and Y depend on the effect.

Try on the device

Reverb: X length, Y colour. Delay: X time, Y feedback. Hold FX + pads during playback for punch-in effects. Holding FX + a group button solos that group. Fader moves recorded with RECORD held become automation. SHIFT + FX opens the output compressor; press + there for sidechain.

Official guide: Effects

12 Automatic song arrangement (song mode)

Tried

Understand

A song list plays scenes in order. Each position plays its scene once, for as long as the scene’s longest pattern, so repeat a position to make a section longer. It is not an audio export.

Try on the device

Hold MAIN + ENTER to open the list; a new list has one position. SHIFT + A adds a position; SHIFT + − / + picks its scene; − / + moves between positions; SHIFT + C cuts one. PLAY starts at the selected position; SHIFT + PLAY from the start.

Official guide: Modes

13 Undo and careful erasing

Tried

Understand

SHIFT + B is undo when the umbrella icon is lit. Erasing notes and deleting a sample file are very different things.

Try on the device

Stopped in MAIN: a brief ERASE + pad removes that pad’s note on the current step. Holding ERASE + pad until TRK blinks removes all of that pad’s notes in the pattern. Holding ERASE + SOUND until SND blinks deletes the sample from the device. Holding ERASE + MAIN for two seconds erases the current scene: DEL means an empty scene that is not in the song was deleted, CLR means its contents were cleared. Practise on disposable work.

Official guide: Play and record

14 What OS 2.5.1 gives you, and where to learn it

Tried

Understand

This course assumes OS 2.5.1. OS 2.0 added song mode, resampling, hands-free sampling and sidechain. OS 2.5 added USB audio, sample reverse, equal-length auto chop, arpeggio and three more KEYS scales. OS 2.5.1 fixes choppy USB audio from Android phones and a crash when cropping long samples. Older OS: update first, see Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II.

Try on the device

Quick test of reverse: select a pad with a sound, press SHIFT + SOUND, press + until TIM shows and turn X until REV shows, then tap the pad: it plays backwards. Turn X back afterwards. Chapter 3 teaches the sampling features and Chapter 4 the playing features. This app cannot detect your OS version.

Official guide: What’s new

15 Copy and paste a bar

Tried

Understand

In MAIN, SHIFT + C copies: press once for the current bar, twice for the whole pattern. SHIFT + D pastes. Useful when a rhythm repeats.

Try on the device

Stopped in MAIN, select the group and go to the bar to copy (SHIFT + − / + jumps a bar). Press SHIFT + C once, move to a bar that already exists in the pattern and press SHIFT + D. Press PLAY to check; SHIFT + B is undo while the umbrella is lit.

Official guide: Play and record

16 Projects, backup and starting fresh

Tried

Understand

A project is one whole composition; the K.O. II holds nine. On a new unit projects 1–5 come with factory content and 6–9 are empty. Commit copies a scene inside a project; it is not a backup.

Try on the device

Hold MAIN, then press and keep holding one of pads 1–9 while you watch the display to change project. Holding MAIN and typing a number also selects a scene, so check the result: in an empty project the pads make no sound. SHIFT + A in MAIN finds the next empty pattern. Back up with the official EP sample tool on a computer over USB-C. There is no harmless reset: holding SHIFT + ERASE while switching on formats the drive, and the official guide says factory sounds cannot be recovered.

Official guide: Workflow

17 Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II: update, back up, stay safe

Tried

Understand

Chapter 0 prepares a new K.O. II before the first beat: updating to OS 2.5.1, backing up with the official EP sample tool, and avoiding the power-on button combinations that erase or lock the device.

Try on the device

Never hold SHIFT + ERASE while switching on: it formats the drive, and the official guide warns “once removed factory sounds can not be recovered!”. Holding MAIN while switching on starts lock mode (LOK on the display), and changes are lost at the next restart. Updates come from the official update utility in a web browser.

18 Chapter 3 · Sample, chop & resample

Tried

Understand

Record your own sounds into empty pads, build a drum kit from your voice, sample your phone, slice a loop across pads, play a sample backwards and resample your beat into one new sample.

Try on the device

In SAMPLE, − / + picks the source: mic, line in, usb or rsp (resample). X sets input level and Y threshold. Hold a pad to record, or press SHIFT + a pad for hands-free sampling and press SAMPLE to stop. SHIFT + SAMPLE opens chop. Chapter 3 takes it one step at a time.

19 Chapter 4 · Play & perform

Tried

Understand

Play the K.O. II as an instrument: live recording, KEYS with a key and scale, arpeggios, note repeat, punch-in FX, fader moves, sidechain, and recording your performance over USB audio.

Try on the device

Hold TIMING and press a pad for note repeat. Select a pad with a sound and press KEYS; hold KEYS and turn X for the key, Y for the scale. Still in KEYS, hold TIMING and press several pads for an arpeggio. While the beat plays, hold FX and press pads for punch-in FX. Chapter 4 takes it one step at a time.
