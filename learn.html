01 Samples, notes and patterns

Tried

Understand

A sample is a sound. A pattern says when each sound plays. A new sample is not in your beat until you place it.

Try on the device

Record a sound in SAMPLE. Then, stopped in MAIN, go to a step, hold RECORD and tap the pad.

02 Groups and scenes

Tried

Understand

Four groups, A to D, each have 12 pads and their own patterns. A scene is the patterns that play together: one section of a song. Sounds and their settings (sample, volume, pitch, trim, play mode) belong to the project, so every scene shares them. Good roles: A drums, B bass, C melody, D your own samples.

Try on the device

Tap a group to select it. Hold a group and press − / + to change its pattern. Hold MAIN and press − / + to change scene. SHIFT + MAIN commits: it saves the scene and moves you into a copy.

Practise it in Chapter 2 · Make it a song: Part 1 · Make the intro.

03 Choosing and balancing sounds

Tried

Understand

SOUND chooses a pad’s sample. Here X sets its volume and Y its pitch. Factory sounds by type: kicks 1–99, snares 100–199, hats 200–299, percussion 300–399, bass 400–499, melodic 500–599.

Try on the device

Select a group, tap a pad, then browse with − / +. SHIFT + − / + jumps by ten. To jump to a number, hold SOUND and type it. Listen before you record.

04 Live recording without panic

Tried

Understand

Stopped, tap RECORD, then press PLAY: four clicks count you in, then recording starts. Quantize (TIMING, then −) snaps what you play to the nearest step.

Try on the device

Play your pads after the count-in. RECORD stops recording and the beat keeps playing. PLAY stops both. SHIFT + B undoes a mistake while the umbrella icon is lit. Practise in a copy of a scene.

05 Precise step recording

Tried

Understand

Stopped in MAIN, − / + moves one step and SHIFT + − / + a whole bar. Hold RECORD and tap a pad to write a note on that step. The display shows bar.beat.step: 1.2.3 is bar 1, beat 2, third step.

Try on the device

Press MAIN and check that playback is stopped. Hold SHIFT and press − until the display shows 1.1.1. Press + once: the position on the display moves one step. Hold RECORD, tap A1 once, then release RECORD: A1 lights on that step. Positions are not sample numbers.

Practise it in Chapter 1 · First beat, with the placement helper: Part 2 · Place the drums.

06 Timing, swing and correction

Tried

Understand

In TIMING, X sets the grid: 16 means 16 steps per bar. Y adds swing. − snaps what you record to the grid, + keeps it as played. These settings change your next recording, not notes already there.

Try on the device

To snap notes already recorded, press SHIFT + TIMING and set the grid with X. Then, stopped, press a pad: all its notes snap to the grid.

10 Trim and envelope safely

Tried

Understand

SHIFT + SOUND opens sound edit, and − / + turns the page. Trim: X start, Y length. Envelope: X fade-in, Y fade-out. Trimming never changes the sample file.

Try on the device

Make small edits and listen. To save your edits to the sound, hold SHIFT + SOUND for two seconds away from the trim page.

No undo: on the trim page, holding SHIFT + SOUND crops the file for good. Holding ERASE + SOUND deletes the sample from the device. See Safety and erasing: Crop a sample, Delete a sample.

Practise it in Chapter 3 · Sample, chop & resample: Part 5 · Shape and reverse a sound in SOUND EDIT.

12 Automatic song arrangement (song mode)

Tried

Understand

The song list plays your scenes in order. Each position plays its scene once, for as long as its longest pattern. Repeat a position to make a section longer.

Try on the device

Hold MAIN and press ENTER to open the list. SHIFT + A adds a position, and SHIFT + − / + picks its scene. − / + moves between positions, and SHIFT + C removes one. SHIFT + PLAY plays from the start.

13 Undo and careful erasing

Tried

Understand

SHIFT + B is undo while the umbrella icon is lit. Erasing notes is not the same as deleting a sample.

Try on the device

Stopped in MAIN, a quick ERASE + pad removes that pad’s note on this step. Hold ERASE + pad until TRK blinks to clear all its notes in the pattern. Practise on a copy.

No undo: holding ERASE + MAIN for two seconds erases the whole scene. Holding ERASE + SOUND until SND blinks deletes the sample from the device. See Safety and erasing: Clear or delete a scene, Delete a sample.

Practise it in Chapter 1 · First beat: Part 6 · Record live and wrap up.

15 Copy and paste a bar

16 Projects, backup and starting fresh

Tried

Understand

A project is one whole song, and the K.O. II holds nine. On a new unit, 1–5 hold factory demos and 6–9 are empty. Commit copies a scene inside a project. It is not a backup.

Try on the device

To change project, hold MAIN, then hold a pad from 1–9 too while you watch the display. A quick press can select a scene instead, so check: in an empty project the pads make no sound. Back up with the EP sample tool on a computer, over USB-C.

No undo: there is no harmless reset. Holding SHIFT + ERASE while switching on formats the drive, and factory sounds cannot be recovered. See Safety and erasing: Format the drive.

Practise it in Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II: Part 5 · Choose your empty project.

20 Coming from another groovebox

Tried

Understand

A project is one whole song, and the K.O. II holds nine. Four groups, A to D, each have 12 pads and their own patterns. A scene is the patterns that play together, and the song list plays scenes in order. These are the K.O. II’s own rules, each with the step that teaches it:

  • Undo: SHIFT + B while the umbrella icon is lit. Owners report one level of undo (Elektronauts), so undo before you change anything else. Make the intro: remove the bass.
  • Commit: SHIFT + MAIN saves the scene and moves you into a copy. It is not a backup. Commit: make a copy.
  • Sounds belong to the project: sample, volume, pitch, trim and play mode are shared by every scene. Commit it as VOICE KIT.
  • MAIN with a pad from 1–9 held opens that project. A quick press can select a scene instead. Open empty project 6.
  • Every recording goes into one shared sound library, and a pad only points to a sample. A crop overwrites the file, so every pad, in every project, that uses it plays the cropped version. Optional: crop the kick.
  • SHIFT + ERASE held while switching on formats the drive, and factory sounds cannot be recovered. With the unit on, the same press opens system settings. Anything else: never format.

Try on the device

Do these six moves from memory, then check each against its step: write a note (Learn the placement move once), erase one note (Make the intro: remove the bass), undo (Optional: keep it or undo it), commit (Commit: make a copy), select a scene (Select BEAT) and add a song position (Add position 2: INTRO). Practise in a copy of a scene.
