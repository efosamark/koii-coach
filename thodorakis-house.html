Thodorakis house

A practical 128 BPM house groove built only from your five samples: C4 C5 C3 C7 C2. Work on a disposable pattern first; do not overwrite a project you want to keep.

Before starting: set tempo to 128 BPM, use a 1/16 grid, and keep swing at 0 for the first pass. Add a little swing later only if the groove needs it.

Sample map

C4 — Kick

Your main low pulse. Keep it short and punchy; avoid stacking it on every 1/16.

C5 — Clap / snare

The backbeat. It should sit on beats 2 and 4, not fight the kick.

C3 — Closed hat

Drives the groove. Start with offbeat 1/8 notes, then add small 1/16 variations.

C7 — Open hat / texture

Use sparingly for lift, transitions and the top of the groove.

C2 — Bass / tonal stab

Your musical element. Use it as a bass answer, chord stab or melodic hook depending on how it sounds.

Step-by-step

  1. Set the canvas. Press TEMPO and set 128. Choose Group A, select an empty pattern, and confirm the timing grid is 1/16.
  2. Add the kick. Select C4 and place it on steps 1, 5, 9 and 13. This is the four-on-the-floor foundation.
  3. Add the backbeat. Select C5 and place it on steps 5 and 13. If it sounds crowded with the kick, lower its volume slightly.
  4. Add offbeat hats. Select C3 and place it on steps 3, 7, 11 and 15. This creates the classic house shuffle feel.
  5. Add a bass answer. Select C2 and place it on steps 1, 4, 9 and 12. Keep it out of the way of the kick at first.
  6. Add movement. Add C3 on step 14, then C7 on step 8 or 16. One open hat per bar is often enough.
  7. Make an 8-bar loop. Copy the basic bar, then change only one thing per bar: remove one hat, move one C2 hit, or add one C7 accent.
  8. Build a intro. For 8 bars, use only C3 and C7. Let the listener hear the space before the drums arrive.
  9. Bring in the groove. Add C4 and C5. Keep C2 out for another 8 bars if you want a stronger build.
  10. Add the hook. Bring in C2 as a repeating 2-bar or 4-bar phrase. Repeat it, then remove it for a breakdown.
  11. Make a breakdown. Remove the kick and clap for 8 bars. Keep C3, C7 and a reduced C2 phrase.
  12. Return with variation. Bring everything back, but change one detail: add an extra C2 hit, open hat, or remove one kick near the end of a phrase.

If it does not groove

  • Too busy: remove C3 from step 15 and use C7 only once per two bars.
  • Too static: move one C2 hit from step 4 to step 6, or from step 12 to step 14.
  • Kick and bass clash: shorten C4, lower C2 volume, or move C2 off the kick steps.
  • No lift: add C7 immediately before a section change, especially on step 16.
  • Feels stiff: try a small amount of swing after the basic groove works.
