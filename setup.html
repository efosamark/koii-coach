CHAPTER 0 / ABOUT 45–60 MINUTES

Set up your K.O. II

Get your K.O. II ready: hear it, read its screen, update and back it up.

Along the way you choose a project for the course and tour the factory sounds. Each part ends with a checkpoint.

You will have: a K.O. II on OS 2.5.1, backed up, with your course project chosen.

Before you start

  • 4 AAA batteries or a USB-C charger
  • Headphones or a speaker
  • A computer with Chrome, a data cable
  • Paper and a pencil

What you need

Your K.O. II (EP-133).

Power: 4 fresh AAA batteries, or a USB-C charger: 5 V, at least 1 A, ideally “USB-IF” compliant.

Sound: the built-in speaker, or headphones or a speaker with a 3.5 mm stereo plug.

For the update and the backup: a computer with internet and Google Chrome, and a USB-C cable that carries data. Some cables only charge.

Paper and a pencil, for project and scene numbers.

  • Never hold SHIFT + ERASE as you switch on.
  • Nothing in Parts 1–7 erases music.
Safety first. Nothing in Parts 1–7 erases music. Switch on with your hands off the buttons:
  • Holding SHIFT + ERASE as you switch on formats the drive: all your work and the factory sounds are erased (Part 8).
  • Holding MAIN as you switch on starts lock mode: LOK shows for a second, and your changes are reset at the next restart.
  • Once the K.O. II is on, SHIFT + ERASE is safe: it opens system settings (Part 7).

Part 1 · Power on and hear it

Goal: switch on and hear the pads through the speaker or headphones.

  1. Fit four AAA batteries

    Lift off the top lid and insert 4 fresh AAA batteries, matching the + and − marks. Put the lid back.

    Check: Each battery’s + and − match the marks.

    Using USB power instead? Connect a USB-C cable from a good charger (5 V, at least 1 A) and skip the batteries.

  2. Switch it on

    Take your hands off every button. Slide the orange power switch at the top right.

    Check: The display lights up with icons and numbers.

  3. Find the battery icon

    On batteries, find the battery icon on the display. It blinks when the batteries are low.

    Check: The battery icon is lit, not blinking.

    If it blinks, fit fresh batteries or switch to USB-C power before the update in Part 3.

  4. Tap pads 1, 2 and 3

    Tap pads 1, 2 and 3.

    Check: Each pad plays a sound through the built-in speaker.

    Why

    A new K.O. II comes with sounds in projects 1–5.

    Not working?

    Silent? You may be in an empty project: hold MAIN, then hold pad 1 as well, release both and tap again. Also check the fader isn’t all the way down.

  5. Optional: plug in headphones

    The input is a 3.5 mm socket on top too: use the output.

    Turn your headphones or speaker down first, then plug them into the 3.5 mm stereo output on top.

    Check: Pads play through them.

  6. Optional: raise the volume slowly

    Tap pads and raise your volume slowly.

    Check: The pads sound clearly through your headphones or speaker.

Switching off? Wait until the floppy icon (saving) goes out, then slide the power switch back.

Checkpoint: the screen is lit and you hear the pads.

If it doesn’t: a dark screen usually means power: check which way round the batteries are, or try fresh ones or USB-C power. BAT as it switches off means the batteries are low. An error code such as E.10? See Part 8.

Part 2 · Read the screen and the button language

Goal: learn the button words, find your way home to MAIN, and read the icons you will check most.

  1. Learn the four button words

    Read the four words below. Every step uses them.

    Press: tap and release. Hold: press and keep it down. X + Y: hold X, press Y, then release both. Then: one after the other. Many buttons and the X and Y knobs change job with the mode, so check which mode you are in.

    Why

    The official guide uses the same words.

  2. MAIN: your home screen

    Press MAIN. If something is playing, press PLAY to stop it.

    Check: The MAIN icon is lit and the display shows a position such as 1.1.1.

    The three numbers are bar, beat and step.

    Why

    MAIN is your home screen: press it whenever you feel lost.

  3. Step through the beat

    Press + three times and watch the last number change.

    Check: The last number goes up by one with each press.

  4. Your first combination: SHIFT + −

    Hold SHIFT, press − until the display shows 1.1.1, then release SHIFT.

    Check: The display shows 1.1.1 again.

    Why

    SHIFT gives buttons a second job. With − or +, it moves a whole bar at a time.

  5. SOUND: the selected pad

    Don’t turn X or Y here yet: in SOUND they change the selected pad’s volume and pitch.

    Press SOUND.

    Check: The SOUND icon lights and the display shows the pad’s sample number.

    Press MAIN to go home.

  6. PLAY lights its icon

    Press PLAY. Press PLAY again to stop.

    Check: The PLAY icon lights while the K.O. II plays.

    If the project has no notes, it plays silence.

  7. Read the icon table

    Read the icon table below.

    Why

    The two you will check most often are the umbrella (undo is possible) and the floppy (saving).

IconWhat it means

Checkpoint: you can tell MAIN from SOUND by the lit icon, and get back to 1.1.1 with SHIFT + −.

If it doesn’t: no position like 1.1.1 on the display? Press MAIN. It keeps moving? Press PLAY to stop.

Part 3 · Update to the latest OS (2.5.1)

Goal: the newest OS on your K.O. II, so every step matches it.

Music on this unit you care about, or bought it second-hand? Back it up first (Part 4), then come back.

  1. Open the update utility

    On your computer, open Google Chrome and go to teenage.engineering/apps/update.

    Why

    The K.O. II doesn’t show its OS version, so the utility reads it for you. It works in Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Brave.

  2. Connect the K.O. II

    With the K.O. II switched on, connect it to the computer with a USB-C cable that carries data.

    Check: The USB MIDI icon on the display lights.

  3. Allow MIDI access

    If the browser asks whether the page may use your MIDI devices, allow it.

    Check: The utility finds your K.O. II.

  4. Read the result

    See what the utility says: a newer version is available, or your K.O. II is up to date.

    If a newer one appears later, install that.

    Why

    The newest version is OS 2.5.1.

  5. Install the update

    Don’t unplug the cable, close the tab or switch the K.O. II off until the utility says it has finished.

    If an update is offered, start it and follow the utility’s on-screen instructions. Then reload the page and let it check again.

    Check: The utility says your K.O. II is up to date.

Checkpoint: the update utility says your K.O. II is up to date.

If it doesn’t: the utility doesn’t find the K.O. II? Try another data cable or USB port, close other music apps and tabs, then reload. Still stuck? Contact teenage engineering support. Update before you go further: song mode needs OS 2.0 and USB audio OS 2.5.

Part 4 · Back up with the EP sample tool

Goal: a copy of your projects and sounds on your computer, before you change anything.

  1. Open the EP sample tool

    Close the update utility tab. With the K.O. II still connected, open teenage.engineering/apps/ep-sample-tool in Chrome. Allow MIDI access if asked.

    Check: The tool shows your K.O. II.

    The tool suggests Chrome or Brave.

  2. Save a backup

    Don’t use its delete or restore features today.

    Use the tool’s backup feature to save your K.O. II to the computer.

    Check: The tool reports that the backup has finished.

    Follow the tool’s own button labels: they can change.

    Why

    Only a backup on a computer keeps your music safe: copying a scene inside the K.O. II is not one. The tool can also back up and restore single projects.

  3. Find the backup file

    Look in your browser’s downloads.

    Check: A new backup file is there.

  4. Copy it to a second place

    Copy it to a second place, such as a USB stick or cloud storage.

    Check: The backup file also appears in the second place.

    Back up again after each chapter you care about, and before any update or troubleshooting. Restoring can replace what is on the K.O. II, so back up before you ever restore.

Checkpoint: a new backup file is on your computer and in one more safe place.

If it doesn’t: the tool doesn’t see the K.O. II? Close the update utility and other music apps, reconnect the cable, reload and allow MIDI. The backup stops halfway? Use a better cable and run it again.

Part 5 · Choose your empty project

Goal: choose an empty project for the course. A project is one song, with 4 groups (A–D) of 12 pads. Out of the box, projects 1–5 hold sounds and 6–9 are empty.

  1. Open factory project 1

    Press MAIN and stop playback if needed. Hold MAIN, then hold pad 1 as well, then release both.

    Check: The display shows P 1 before you release.

  2. Tap A, then A1, A2 and A3

    Tap A to select group A, then tap A1, A2 and A3.

    Check: Each pad plays a sound.

  3. Open empty project 6

    Hold MAIN, then hold pad 6 as well, then release both.

    Check: The display shows P 6 before you release.

  4. Check every group is silent

    Tap A, then A1, A2 and A3 again, then tap B, C and D and try a few pads in each.

    Check: Silence everywhere.

    Why

    An empty project has no sounds on any pad.

  5. PLAY: silence

    Press PLAY. Press PLAY again to stop.

    Check: Nothing plays.

  6. Write PROJECT 6 = COURSE

    Write PROJECT 6 = COURSE on your paper.

    Chapters 3, 5 and 6 use projects 7, 8 and 9, so keep 7 and 8 empty. Until then, project 9 is your scratch project. Leave factory projects 1–5 as they are.

    Why

    Chapters 1 and 2 build your beat and song here. A new unit has only four empty projects, so Chapter 7 shows how to back up and reuse one.

Checkpoint: project 1 plays sounds, project 6 is silent everywhere, and PROJECT 6 is on your paper.

If it doesn’t: the pads still sound after MAIN + pad 6? You may have switched scene instead of project. Hold MAIN first, then pad 6 as well, and release both only when the display shows P 6. Music in 6–9 on a second-hand unit? Back it up (Part 4), then use any project whose pads are all silent.

Part 6 · Tour the factory sound library

Goal: hear each kind of factory sound and learn its number range, in scratch project 9 so project 6 stays empty.

Sample numbersFactory sounds stored there
1–99Kicks
100–199Snares
200–299Hi-hats
300–399Percussion
400–499Bass
500–599Melodic sounds

A new K.O. II holds over 300 factory samples in these ranges.

  1. Open scratch project 9

    Hold MAIN, then hold pad 9 as well, then release both. Tap a few pads: all silent.

    Check: The display shows P 9 before you release.

  2. SOUND: select A1

    Press SOUND, tap A, then tap A1.

    Check: The SOUND icon lights. A1 is still silent.

  3. Browse the kicks

    Press + and tap A1. Repeat a few times.

    Check: The number goes up and A1 plays a different kick each time.

    Numbers 1–99 are kicks.

  4. Jump ten at a time

    Hold SHIFT and press + to jump ten numbers at a time, then step with − and + alone.

    Check: The number jumps by ten, then by one.

  5. Jump to the snares

    Hold SOUND, type 1, 0, 0 on the pads, release SOUND and tap A1.

    Check: You hear a snare.

  6. Try the other categories

    Do the same with 2, 0, 0 (hi-hats), 3, 0, 0 (percussion), 4, 0, 0 (bass) and 5, 0, 0 (melodic sounds).

    Check: A1 plays each kind of sound in turn.

    Use − and + to hear a few neighbours each time.

  7. Choose your favourite kick

    Still in SOUND with group A, tap A1 and browse to your favourite kick.

    Check: A1 plays your favourite kick, numbered 1–99.

  8. Pick a snare and a hi-hat

    Tap A2 and choose a snare, tap A3 and choose a hi-hat. Write the three numbers on your paper.

    Check: A2 plays a snare and A3 a hi-hat.

  9. Play your mini kit

    Press MAIN and tap 1, 2, 3, 2 like a beat.

    Check: You hear kick, snare, hi-hat, then snare.

    Chapter 1 starts from samples 001, 100 and 200. Swap in your favourites there if you like.

Careful in SOUND: holding ERASE + SOUND until SND blinks permanently removes the selected sample from the K.O. II. You don’t need it here.

Tip: in SOUND, hold a pad to see its sample’s name, if it has one.

Checkpoint: each number range plays the kind of sound in the table, and three favourite numbers are on your paper.

If it doesn’t: no sound? Check the SOUND icon is lit and you tap the pad you are browsing. Many numbers silent, or sounds that don’t match the table? The library may have been changed or erased: see Part 8.

Part 7 · System settings worth knowing

Goal: change one system setting in the menu, then change it back with a number code.

SHIFT + ERASE while the K.O. II is on opens system settings: safe. Held while you switch on, the same two buttons format the drive (Part 8).

  1. Open the system settings

    In project 9, press SHIFT + ERASE.

    Check: The SYSTEM icon lights and the display shows a short settings word.

    The setting groups are called mid, syn, pad, seq and smp.

  2. Open the seq group

    Press + or − until the display shows seq, then press the ENTER pad.

    Check: The display shows a setting inside seq.

    Why

    The menu names each setting, so you see what you change.

  3. Turn the metronome on (400)

    Press + or − until it shows met and press ENTER. Press + or − until it shows on and press ENTER.

    Check: The display showed on, not rec or cnt, when you pressed ENTER.

    Why

    This is setting 400: the metronome clicks while playing as well as while recording.

  4. Leave, and raise the click

    Press MAIN to leave. Press TEMPO and turn Y up a little for the click volume. X changes the tempo, so leave it.

    Check: The SYSTEM icon goes out.

  5. Hear the click

    Press PLAY. Press PLAY to stop.

    Check: You hear a click on every beat and the metronome icon blinks in time.

  6. The fast way: type 401

    Press SHIFT + ERASE, type 4, 0, 1 on the pads, press ENTER, then press MAIN.

    Check: After MAIN, the SYSTEM icon goes out.

  7. PLAY: no click now

    Press PLAY. Press PLAY to stop.

    Check: No click: 401, the default, clicks only while you record.

  8. Optional: the course picks

    Set any course pick from the table below the same way: SHIFT + ERASE, the three digits, ENTER, then MAIN.

    Check: After MAIN, the SYSTEM icon goes out.

    Change one setting at a time and check it before the next.

CodesSettingCourse pick
300 · 301 · 302Pad velocity off (default) · high, for a soft touch · low, for vigorous playing300: every tap plays at the same level while you learn.
310 · 311Sample audition on · offLeave as it is.

Checkpoint: with 400 you heard a click while playing, with 401 you didn’t, and the SYSTEM icon is off.

If it doesn’t: no click with 400? In TEMPO, turn Y higher. Lost in the menu? SHIFT + ENTER goes back one level and MAIN leaves. Typed the wrong code? Type the right one: none of these codes erases music.

Part 8 · If something is wrong: empty library, error codes and the format

Goal: know what to do if sounds are missing or the display shows an error, and why a new K.O. II never needs a format.

  1. Library complete? Move on

    If Part 6 played every category, your library is complete: tick this and move on.

    Not working?

    Projects 1–5 silent, and most sample numbers empty? The library has probably been erased, for example by a previous owner’s format. Chapter 3, Part 2 “Build a drum kit from your voice” records your own kick, snare and hi-hat with the built-in mic onto A1, A2 and A3. The EP sample tool can also restore the factory sounds: back up first, because restoring can replace what is on the K.O. II.

  2. Error code? Look it up

    If the display shows an error code when you switch on, look it up.

    E.01, E.02 and E.03 mean the unit needs repair: contact teenage engineering support, and don’t format. E.05, E.10, E.11 and E.12 are file-system errors, fixed by the format in the warning below. Update the OS first if you can: updates keep fixing bugs that may cause them.

  3. Anything else: never format

    For anything else, such as silence, wrong sounds, a messy project or a mistake, never format.

    Undo with SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit, choose another empty project, or restore your backup with the EP sample tool.

    Check: The mistake is gone and your sounds are still there.

Warning: SHIFT + ERASE held while you switch on formats the drive. It removes all your work and all factory sounds. Factory sounds cannot be recovered.
  • Use it only to fix errors E.05, E.10, E.11 and E.12. A new K.O. II never needs it.
  • Back up first with the EP sample tool, if the K.O. II still connects.
  • You’ll recognise it: the display shows FMT for about 10 seconds, then the K.O. II starts up empty.

Checkpoint: you know which errors need a repair, which need a format, and that a new K.O. II needs neither.

If it doesn’t: an error not listed here, or a format that doesn’t help? Contact teenage engineering support.

Part 9 · Checklist: ready for Chapter 1

Goal: tick each item only when it is true for your K.O. II.

  1. Ready check: updated

    The update utility says your K.O. II is up to date: OS 2.5.1 or newer.

  2. Ready check: backed up

    Your backup file is on your computer and in a second place.

  3. Ready check: project 6

    Hold MAIN, then hold pad 6 as well, and release both. Tap a few pads in A, B, C and D: all silent.

    Check: Nothing plays in any group.

  4. Ready check: silence, and paper

    Press PLAY: silence. Press PLAY to stop. PROJECT 6 = COURSE is on your paper.

    Check: Nothing plays while the PLAY icon is lit.

  5. Set-up done: your moves

    Read the six moves below, then try one from memory.

    • Home: press MAIN. PLAY starts and stops.
    • To the start: hold SHIFT and press − until the display shows 1.1.1.
    • Change project: hold MAIN, hold a pad 1–9 as well, then release both.
    • Browse sounds: SOUND, tap the pad, then − / + (SHIFT + + jumps ten). Hold SOUND and type a number to jump there.
    • System settings, with the unit already on: SHIFT + ERASE, three digits, ENTER, then MAIN.
    • Undo: SHIFT + B while the umbrella is lit.

  6. Open Chapter 1

    Leave the K.O. II on in project 6, keep paper and pencil nearby, and open Chapter 1 · First beat.

Checkpoint: all four items are ticked. You are ready for your first beat.

If it doesn’t: one item isn’t true yet? Go back to Part 3 (update), Part 4 (backup) or Part 5 (project).
