No sound

A pad is silent

Load a sound first: press SOUND, select the group and pad, and choose a sample with − / +. A number that plays nothing may be an empty slot: press + for the next one. Still quiet? Turn up X (amplitude) in SOUND. In MAIN, check the fader is not all the way down: it sets the selected group’s level.

Official guide: Modes

My sample will not start recording

Lower the threshold with Y in SAMPLE: a high threshold can stop a quiet sound from starting the recording. Check the input source with − / + and the level with X. “disk low” means there is not enough space for 20 seconds: back up with the EP sample tool, then delete samples you don’t need. Hands-free sampling (SHIFT + a pad) records until you press SAMPLE.

Official guide: Functions

My sample is too quiet or noisy

In SAMPLE, X sets the input level and Y the threshold.

  • Too quiet: move closer to the mic or turn the source up, then record again. Raise X a little if needed.
  • Hiss: turn the source up and X down, and record somewhere quiet. For a phone or computer, use line in or USB rather than the mic.
  • Distorted: lower X or the source volume.
  • Start cut off: lower Y. Starts on background noise: raise Y.
  • Dull or lo-fi: set system setting 502 to go back to the high recording rate.
  • Quiet when you tap the pad: raise X (amplitude) in SOUND.

Official guide: Functions

USB audio is not detected or sounds choppy

Use a USB-C cable that carries data, not a charge-only one, and select the K.O. II as the audio input or output on the computer or phone. USB audio needs OS 2.5 or later, and OS 2.5.1 fixes choppy audio from Android devices, so update first. To sample from USB, press SAMPLE, then + until usb shows, and hold a pad. To hear USB input through the K.O. II as well, set system setting 511. The default, 510, sends it to the sampler only. Still choppy? Close other audio apps and try another cable or port.

Official guide: How to

The arpeggio does not play

Set the sound’s play mode to oneshot or legato, not key: press SHIFT + SOUND and, on the sound page, turn X until the display shows one (oneshot) or leg (legato), not key. Then select a pad with a sound, press KEYS, hold TIMING and press several pads. They play one after another, in the order you pressed them. Arpeggio needs OS 2.5 or later.

Timing and notes

My taps do not repeat

A tap only plays the pad. To write a note, stop, press MAIN, find the position, hold RECORD and tap the pad: it lights on that step. Live: tap RECORD, then PLAY for a count-in.

Official guide: Play and record

The beat has extra hits

Press SHIFT + B (undo) while the umbrella icon is lit. Or stop, go to the unwanted note in MAIN and briefly press ERASE + its pad. Keep it brief: holding ERASE + pad until TRK blinks clears all of that pad’s notes. Never use ERASE + SOUND: it deletes the sample file.

Official guide: Play and record

The snare is in the wrong place

Go to the misplaced snare’s position and briefly press ERASE + A2. Chapter 1’s snares sit at 1.2.1 and 1.4.1 (grid steps 5 and 13), with TIMING at 16 in 4/4. Check the display before you hold RECORD + A2.

Official guide: Get started

My bass loops too soon

Before writing a longer phrase, add a bar: select B, stop, then in MAIN hold RECORD and press +. Each group has its own pattern length.

Official guide: Play and record

− and + change the wrong thing

What they change depends on the mode, and on what you hold:

  • SOUND: the sample.
  • Stopped MAIN: the position.
  • Holding a group: that group’s pattern.
  • Holding MAIN: the scene.
  • Holding RECORD: the pattern length.
  • Song list: the song position.

Changed a group’s pattern by accident? Hold the group and press the other button to go back.

Official guide: Modes

TIMING did not fix old notes

For recorded notes, press SHIFT + TIMING (timing correct) and set the interval with X. While stopped, press the pad to snap its notes to the grid. TIMING settings apply only while you record.

Official guide: Functions

Chopping replaced the sounds on my pads

Press SHIFT + B (undo) at once if the umbrella is lit, or load the sounds again in SOUND by number. Auto chop resets the pad assignments of the group you chop into. Choose an empty group, or one whose pads you don’t need. Chapter 3 chops into the empty group C, as D holds your recordings, and Chapter 7 keeps D for your vocals.

Official guide: Functions

Stuck in a mode

One sound plays at many pitches

KEYS mode is on: the piano icon is lit. Press KEYS once more: the piano icon goes out and each pad plays its own sound again. Owners report that pressing KEYS again leaves KEYS mode (Reddit (opens in a new tab)).

Official guide: Play and record (keys)

My taps keep repeating on their own

RECORD was on: stop and press SHIFT + B (undo) while the umbrella is lit. The RECORD icon blinks while recording is armed: tap RECORD again if it still blinks.

Official guide: Play and record

Lost in system settings

The SYSTEM icon is lit while system settings are open. SHIFT + ENTER goes back one level and MAIN leaves. Typed the wrong code? Type the right one: none of the codes in the settings table erases music.

Official guide: System

X or Y changes the wrong thing

What they change depends on the mode, and on what you hold:

  • SOUND: X the volume, Y the pitch.
  • Sound edit: that page’s settings, such as the trim start (X) and length (Y).
  • TEMPO: X the BPM, Y the metronome volume.
  • TIMING: X the grid, Y swing.
  • SAMPLE: X the input level, Y the threshold.
  • Holding KEYS: X the key, Y the scale.
  • FX: the effect’s two settings.
  • MAIN: X the input gain, Y how much input goes to the effect.

X or Y changed volume or pitch in KEYS mode? Keep KEYS held while you turn.

Official guide: Modes · Effects

A pad plays backwards, stretched or cut short

Pad settings belong to the project and stay until you change them: Chapter 3 leaves A4 reversed and B1 stretched in project 7. Reset the pad in SOUND EDIT (SHIFT + SOUND, then − / + turns the page), as in Chapter 3, Parts 5 and 8. Backwards or stretched: the time page (tim), where X sets reverse or the stretch. Cut short: check oneshot on the sound page, then the length (Y) on the trim page (TRI).

Projects and scenes

My project is not empty

Open an empty project: hold MAIN, press pad 6, 7, 8 or 9 and keep both held until the display changes. On a new unit, projects 1–5 hold factory demos and 6–9 are empty. In an empty project, group A’s pads make no sound and PLAY gives silence. Pads still play? MAIN with a number also selects a scene: try again, holding the pad longer. Chapters 1, 2 and 4 use project 6, Chapter 3 uses 7, Chapter 5 uses 8 and Chapter 6 uses 9, the scratch project from Chapter 0. Chapter 7 reuses the one you need least, usually 7. Music in 6–9 on a second-hand unit? Back it up, then use any project whose pads are all silent. Write that number as COURSE, and in Chapters 1, 2 and 4 use it wherever a step says project 6. All nine in use? Back up with the EP sample tool first. Then press SHIFT + MAIN for a fresh scene, select each group A, B, C and D in turn and press SHIFT + A for its next empty pattern. Loading new sounds there changes that project’s pads.

Official guide: Get started

Which scene am I in?

Hold MAIN: the display shows the scene number, and − / + selects another. After SHIFT + MAIN you are in the new copy, and the scene you left keeps the finished version. Write each number and its name on paper as you go: DRUMS, BEAT, INTRO…

Official guide: Modes

The song is too short or has extra positions

Open the song list (MAIN + ENTER) and count the positions with − / +. Extra position: select it and press SHIFT + C. Missing one: SHIFT + A adds a position, and SHIFT + − / + picks its scene. Each position plays its scene once, for as long as the scene’s longest pattern.

Official guide: Modes

Scene changes land at the wrong moment

Make scene changes wait for the end of the bar: press SHIFT + ERASE, type 4, 1, 1, press ENTER, then MAIN. Type 4, 1, 2 instead to wait for the end of the pattern. Then pick the next scene a little before the bar or pattern ends. The default, 410, switches at once: type it the same way to go back. Code not accepted? Check that system settings are open (SHIFT + ERASE). On an older OS, update first with Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II.

Official guide: System

I erased or changed the wrong thing

Press SHIFT + B straight away: undo works while the umbrella icon is lit. Owners report one level of undo (Elektronauts). Umbrella off? Write the notes again from the step, or go back to an earlier copy of the scene (hold MAIN + − / +). Keep erase presses short: holding ERASE + pad until TRK blinks clears that pad’s notes, ERASE + a group until PTN blinks clears patterns, and ERASE + MAIN for two seconds erases the scene (DEL or CLR).

Official guide: Play and record

Storage and backup

How do I export or back up?

Back up with the EP sample tool (teenage.engineering/apps/ep-sample-tool) on a computer over USB-C. It backs up, restores and transfers samples. Back up before updating, deleting samples or formatting. SHIFT + MAIN (commit) copies a scene inside the project: it is not a backup. To record audio, connect the K.O. II to a computer or phone and select it as the audio input there. USB audio needs OS 2.5 or later.

Official guide: Functions

My changes disappeared after switching off

Switch off, then on again without holding any button. If LOK showed for about a second at switch-on, MAIN was held and the K.O. II started in lock mode. Lock mode prevents changes to projects, patterns, scenes, pads and settings, and resets any changes on restart. There is no unlock setting: restarting is the only way out.

Official guide: System

Factory sounds are missing after a format

Made a backup with the EP sample tool before the format? Restore it with the same tool. No backup? The guide warns that “once removed factory sounds can not be recovered!”, but the EP sample tool’s page says you can “also use it to restore factory sounds”. If you can’t find the option, ask teenage engineering support. A restore can replace what is on the K.O. II, so back up first. A format starts when SHIFT + ERASE is held at switch-on, so switch on with your hands off the buttons. Meanwhile, Chapter 3 (Sample, chop & resample) shows how to record your own sounds onto pads.

Official guide: Erase drive

I want to reset the K.O. II

To start fresh without losing anything, open an empty project (hold MAIN and one of pads 6–9). To erase one scene, hold ERASE + MAIN for two seconds. There is no harmless factory reset: holding SHIFT + ERASE while switching on formats the drive, removing all your work and all factory sounds, and factory sounds cannot be recovered. Format only when the display shows a file-system error code (E.05, E.10, E.11 or E.12), and back up with the EP sample tool first.

Official guide: System

OS and new features

How do I check or update the OS?

Update from your web browser with teenage engineering’s update utility (teenage.engineering/apps/update), with the K.O. II connected by USB-C. Back up with the EP sample tool first. Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II walks through it. The display doesn’t show the OS version, but the update utility and the EP sample tool both do. The chapters are written for OS 2.5.1.

Official guide: System

Where did USB or reverse go?

USB audio and sample reverse arrived in OS 2.5. Missing? Update your K.O. II: see “How do I check or update the OS?” or Chapter 0 · Set up your K.O. II. Reverse: select the pad, press SHIFT + SOUND, press + until tim shows and turn X until rev shows. USB sampling: connect the computer or phone with a USB-C cable, press SAMPLE, then + until usb shows.

Official guide: What’s new

Where are the new OS 2.5 features?

Chapter 3 (Sample, chop & resample) covers sampling: USB sampling, equal-length auto chop (EQU), reverse, hands-free sampling and resampling (RSP). Chapter 4 (Play & perform) covers playing: KEYS key and scale, arpeggio, note repeat, punch-in FX and sidechain. It also records your performance into a phone or computer over USB audio. The guide’s What’s new page lists every change.
