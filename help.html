24 troubleshooting topics.

No sound

A pad is silent

In SOUND select the right group and pad, then browse a sample with − / + and check its amplitude with X. Pads in an empty project have no sound until you load one. If a sample number plays nothing, that slot may be empty: press + to the next one. In MAIN the fader sets the selected group’s level, so check it is not all the way down.

Official guide: Modes

My sample will not start recording

In SAMPLE check the input source (− / +), X input level and Y threshold. A high threshold can stop a quiet sound from starting the capture. Hands-free sampling (SHIFT + a pad) keeps recording until you press SAMPLE. “disk low” on the display means there is not enough space left to record 20 seconds: back up with the official EP sample tool, then delete samples you do not need. For level problems, see “My sample is too quiet or noisy”.

Official guide: Functions

My sample is too quiet or noisy

In SAMPLE, X sets the input level and Y the threshold. Too quiet: move the sound closer to the mic, turn up the phone or synth feeding line in, or raise X a little and record again. Hiss or noise: turn the source up and X down, use line in or USB instead of the mic for phones and computers, and record somewhere quiet. Distorted: lower X or the source volume. Start cut off: lower Y. Recording starts on background noise: raise Y. Sounds dull or lo-fi: system setting 502 is the default high recording rate; 500 and 501 are lower. Afterwards, X in SOUND (amplitude) sets how loud the pad plays.

Official guide: Functions

USB audio is not detected or sounds choppy

USB audio needs OS 2.5 or later, and OS 2.5.1 fixes choppy USB audio when streaming from Android devices (the official release notes name the Pixel 9), so update first. Use a USB-C cable that carries data, not a charge-only cable. On the computer or phone, select the K.O. II as the audio input, output or both. To sample from USB, press SAMPLE, then + until usb shows, and hold a pad. By default USB input goes to the sampler only (system setting 510); setting 511 adds live monitoring, so you hear it through the K.O. II. Still choppy? Close other audio apps and try another cable or port.

Official guide: How to

The arpeggio does not play

Arpeggio needs OS 2.5 or later. Select a pad with a sound, press KEYS, then hold TIMING and press several pads: they play one after another in the order you pressed them. The official guide says the sound’s play mode must be oneshot or legato: press SHIFT + SOUND and, on the sound page, turn X to oneshot or legato rather than key (check the display). Then press KEYS and try again.

Timing and notes

My taps do not repeat

You are auditioning, not sequencing. Stop playback, press MAIN, navigate to the position, then hold RECORD and tap the pad: it lights on that step. For live playing, tap RECORD, then PLAY for a count-in.

Official guide: Play and record

The beat has extra hits

Press SHIFT + B (undo) while the umbrella icon is lit. Or, stopped in MAIN, go to the unwanted note and briefly press ERASE + its pad. Holding ERASE + pad until TRK blinks clears all of that pad’s notes, so keep the press short. Never use ERASE + SOUND: it deletes the sample file.

Official guide: Play and record

The snare is in the wrong place

Our snares sit at 1.2.1 and 1.4.1 (grid steps 5 and 13) with TIMING at 16 in 4/4. Check the display before holding RECORD + A2, and erase a misplaced one with a brief ERASE + A2 at its position.

Official guide: Get started

My bass loops too soon

Each group has its own pattern length. Select B, stop, and in MAIN hold RECORD and press + to add a bar before writing a longer phrase.

Official guide: Play and record

− and + change the wrong thing

Check the mode and what you are holding. SOUND: − / + choose samples. Stopped MAIN: they move between positions. Holding a group: they change that group’s pattern. Holding MAIN: they change scene. Holding RECORD: pattern length. Song list: song positions. Changed a group’s pattern by accident? Hold the group and press the opposite button to go back.

Official guide: Modes

TIMING did not fix old notes

Ordinary TIMING settings apply when you record, not afterwards. For notes already recorded, press SHIFT + TIMING (timing correct), set the interval with X, then, while stopped, press the pad to quantize its notes.

Official guide: Functions

Chopping replaced the sounds on my pads

When you choose the group for an auto chop, the official guide warns that this “will reset any pad assignments”, so pick an empty group or one you are happy to write over. Chop into a group whose pads you don’t need: chapter 07 chops into the empty group C because group D holds your own recordings there, and chapter 11 keeps D for your vocals. Lost sounds you wanted? Try SHIFT + B (undo) at once if the umbrella is lit, or load them again in SOUND by number.

Projects and scenes

My project is not empty

On a new unit projects 1–5 hold factory demos and 6–9 are completely empty. Hold MAIN, then press pad 6, 7, 8 or 9 and keep both held while you watch the display; release once it changes. Check: tap a few pads in group A. In an empty project they make no sound, and PLAY gives silence. Pads still play sounds? You selected a scene instead, because holding MAIN and typing a number also selects a scene: try again, keeping the pad held longer. The course uses 6 for chapters 01, 02 and 08, 7 for 07, 8 for 09 and 9 (the scratch project from 00) for 10; chapter 11 then reuses the project you need least, usually 7. All nine projects in use? Back up first with the official EP sample tool, then press SHIFT + MAIN for a fresh scene and, for each group A, B, C and D in turn, select it and press SHIFT + A (next empty pattern). Loading new sounds there changes that project’s pads.

Official guide: Get started

Which scene am I in?

Hold MAIN: the display shows the scene number, and − / + selects another. After SHIFT + MAIN you are in the new copy, and the scene you left keeps the finished version. Write each number and its name (DRUMS, BEAT, INTRO…) on paper as you go.

Official guide: Modes

The song is too short or has extra positions

Each song position plays its scene once, for as long as the scene’s longest pattern. The chapter 02 plan has 10 positions, 16 bars, about 43 seconds at 90 BPM. Open the list (MAIN + ENTER) and count positions with − / +. Extra position: select it and press SHIFT + C. Missing one: SHIFT + A adds a position and SHIFT + − / + picks its scene.

Official guide: Modes

Scene changes land at the wrong moment

By default a scene you pick starts at once (system setting 410). Press SHIFT + ERASE, type 4, 1, 1 and press ENTER so changes wait for the end of the bar, or type 4, 1, 2 so they wait for the end of the pattern, then press MAIN. Now pick the next scene a little before the bar or pattern ends. Type 410 the same way to switch back. Code not accepted? Check that system settings are open (SHIFT + ERASE); on an older OS, update first (see 00 Start here).

Official guide: System

I erased or changed the wrong thing

Press SHIFT + B straight away: undo works while the umbrella icon is lit. If it is not lit, write the notes again from the step text, or go back to an earlier committed scene (hold MAIN + − / +). Keep erase presses short: holding ERASE + pad until TRK blinks clears that pad’s notes, holding ERASE + a group until PTN blinks clears patterns, and holding ERASE + MAIN for two seconds erases the scene (DEL or CLR).

Storage and backup

How do I export or back up?

Commit (SHIFT + MAIN) makes a scene copy inside the project, not a backup. Back up with the official EP sample tool (teenage.engineering/apps/ep-sample-tool) on a computer over USB-C: it has a backup and restore feature and also transfers samples. Back up before updating, deleting samples or formatting. To record audio, plug the K.O. II into a computer or phone and select it as the audio input there (USB audio, OS 2.5 and later). The coach has no hardware connection.

Official guide: Functions

My changes disappeared after switching off

If LOK showed on the display for about a second when you switched on, the K.O. II started in lock mode because MAIN was held during start-up. Lock mode prevents changes to projects, patterns, scenes, pads and settings, and any changes reset when the device restarts. Switch off, then on again without holding any button. The official guide does not describe a separate unlock step.

Official guide: System

Factory sounds are missing after a format

Holding SHIFT + ERASE while switching on formats the drive: FMT shows for about 10 seconds, then the K.O. II starts with all content removed. The official guide warns this removes all your work and all factory sounds, and “once removed factory sounds can not be recovered!”. If you backed up the unit with the official EP sample tool beforehand, restore that backup with the same tool. Without a backup, open the EP sample tool on a computer: its official page says you can “also use it to restore factory sounds”, although the guide itself does not describe how, so if you can’t find the option, ask teenage engineering support. A restore can replace what is on the K.O. II, so back up first. Meanwhile, make your own kit: chapter 07 (Sample, chop & resample) shows how to record and chop your own sounds onto pads.

Official guide: Erase drive

I want to reset the K.O. II

There is no harmless factory reset. Holding SHIFT + ERASE while switching on formats the drive, and the official guide warns it removes all your work and all factory sounds, which cannot be recovered. To start fresh without losing anything, use an empty project (hold MAIN and one of pads 6–9) or erase a single scene by holding ERASE + MAIN for two seconds. The guide calls for formatting only when the display shows a file-system error code (E.05, E.10, E.11 or E.12). If you ever must format, back up with the official EP sample tool first.

OS and new features

How do I check or update the OS?

This course is written for OS 2.5.1 (official release 3 July 2026). The official downloads page says to “update directly from your web browser with our update-utility” (teenage.engineering/apps/update), with the K.O. II connected by USB-C. Back up with the official EP sample tool first. The official guide does not describe a way to show the installed version on the device itself. Chapter 00 Start here walks through the update.

Official guide: System

Where did USB or reverse go?

USB audio and sample reverse arrived in OS 2.5, and this course assumes OS 2.5.1. If they are missing, your K.O. II runs an older OS: update it (see “How do I check or update the OS?” and 00 Start here). This app cannot detect or update your firmware. Reverse: select the pad, press SHIFT + SOUND, press + until TIM shows and turn X until REV shows. USB sampling: connect the computer or phone with a USB-C cable, press SAMPLE, then + until usb shows.

Official guide: What’s new

Where are the new OS 2.5 features?

This course assumes OS 2.5.1. Chapter 07 (Sample, chop & resample) covers the sampling side: USB sampling, equal-length auto chop (EQU), reverse, hands-free sampling and resampling (RSP). Chapter 08 (Play & perform) covers the playing side: KEYS key and scale, arpeggio, note repeat, punch-in FX and sidechain. Chapter 02 already used song mode. Chapter 08 also records your performance into a phone or computer over USB audio; if that fails, see “USB audio is not detected or sounds choppy”. The official what’s new page lists every change.
