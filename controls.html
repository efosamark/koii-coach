The controls

Schematic of the K.O. II’s top, after teenage engineering’s photos: not to scale, and not an official drawing.

The controls sit where they are on the unit, but this is not a working emulator. The small words above the pads are what each pad picks when you hold FADER.

First ask: which mode?

  • SOUND chooses and shapes a pad’s sound.
  • MAIN handles patterns, playback and arrangement.
  • TEMPO changes pace and metronome volume.
  • TIMING handles recording grid and swing.
  • SAMPLE captures audio, not note events.
X, Y and − / + change jobs with context.

Official guide: Modes

Every control

MAIN

Home and arrangement

Navigate and record patterns; hold MAIN with − / + to select scenes. SHIFT + MAIN commits a scene copy. Hold MAIN and press ENTER to open the song list. Hold MAIN and one of pads 1–9 to change project; holding MAIN and typing a number also selects a scene, so watch the display. Holding ERASE + MAIN for two seconds erases the scene. MAIN returns here from any page.

Official guide: Modes

SOUND

Select a sample

Choose the group and pad first. − / + browses samples (SHIFT + − / + jumps by ten), or hold SOUND and type the sample number on the pads. X amplitude, Y pitch. SHIFT + SOUND opens sound edit.

Official guide: Modes

TEMPO

Tempo and metronome

X sets BPM; Y the recording metronome volume. Hold TEMPO and type a number for an exact BPM. Our recipe uses 90 BPM. MAIN + TEMPO changes the time signature; SHIFT + TEMPO starts a performance loop.

Official guide: Modes

TIMING

Grid and groove

X note interval (16 in this course); Y swing. − quantize; + free time. SHIFT + TIMING corrects notes already recorded. Hold TIMING and a pad for note repeat; in KEYS, hold TIMING and several pads for an arpeggio.

Official guide: Functions

SAMPLE

Capture audio

− / + chooses the input source (mic, line in, usb, rsp resample). X input level, Y threshold. Hold a pad to capture, release to stop; SHIFT + a pad samples hands-free until you press SAMPLE. SHIFT + SAMPLE opens chop.

Official guide: Functions

KEYS

Pitch keyboard

Select one sample, then KEYS: the pads play it at 12 pitches. Held KEYS with − / + changes octave; held KEYS with X sets the key and with Y the scale. Hold TIMING and several pads for an arpeggio (oneshot or legato sounds).

Official guide: Play and record

FX

Effects

− / + chooses the shared master effect. The fader sets the selected group’s send. Hold FX + pads for punch-in effects; while playing, hold FX + a group button to solo that group. SHIFT + FX opens the output compressor; + there switches to sidechain.

Official guide: Effects

Fader

Contextual slider

Normally group level; in FX the group’s send. Hold FADER + a printed pad assignment to choose another parameter. Hold RECORD while moving it to record automation.

Official guide: Play and record

Knob X

Contextual knob

SOUND: amplitude. TEMPO: BPM. TIMING: interval. SAMPLE: input level. MAIN: input gain. KEYS held: key. Check the mode before turning; hold SHIFT while turning for finer steps.

Official guide: Modes

Knob Y

Contextual knob

SOUND: pitch. TEMPO: metronome. TIMING: swing. SAMPLE: threshold. Trim page: playback length. MAIN: FX send level. KEYS held: scale.

Official guide: Modes

Minus

− / +

In SOUND: sample choice. In stopped MAIN: positions (SHIFT + − / + moves a bar). Holding a group changes its pattern; holding MAIN changes scene; with RECORD held, pattern length.

Official guide: Modes

Plus

− / +

Same contextual navigation. With RECORD held, adds a bar to the pattern. In the song list, moves between positions. In SAMPLE, chooses the input source.

Official guide: Modes

SHIFT

Secondary functions

SHIFT + A next empty pattern; SHIFT + B undo; SHIFT + C copy; SHIFT + D paste; SHIFT + MAIN commit; SHIFT + SOUND sound edit; SHIFT + SAMPLE chop; SHIFT + TIMING timing correct; SHIFT + FX output and sidechain; SHIFT + ERASE system settings. Never hold SHIFT + ERASE while switching on: that formats the drive.

Official guide: Modes

ERASE

Use with care

Stopped in MAIN: brief ERASE + pad removes the note on this step; hold until TRK blinks to clear all that pad’s notes in the pattern. ERASE + MAIN held for two seconds erases the scene (DEL or CLR). ERASE + SOUND permanently deletes the selected sample. SHIFT + ERASE held while switching on formats the drive and removes the factory sounds for good.

Official guide: Play and record

RECORD

Record events

Tap, then PLAY: live recording with a count-in. Hold with a pad: step entry. Hold with +: add a bar. Tap during recording to stop recording but keep playing. Hold while moving the fader to record automation.

Official guide: Play and record

PLAY

Start / stop

Starts and stops playback. In the song list, SHIFT + PLAY plays from the start. Stop before position-by-position step entry.

Official guide: Play and record

Group A

Group A

A bank of 12 pads with its own patterns. The recipe uses A1 kick, A2 snare and A3 hat. SHIFT + A: next empty pattern in MAIN; adds a position in the song list.

Official guide: Modes

Group B

Group B

Another bank. The recipe uses B1 for bass. SHIFT + B is undo when the umbrella icon is lit.

Official guide: Play and record

Group C

Group C

Melody bank in Chapter 2, with C1 as the melody sound. SHIFT + C copies (in MAIN: once for the bar, twice for the pattern).

Official guide: Play and record

Group D

Group D

The official guide suggests keeping D free for your own samples. SHIFT + D pastes what you copied.

Official guide: Get started

Number pads

Performance pads

Tap to audition; held RECORD + pad writes on a step, and a lit pad has a note on this step. In SAMPLE hold to record audio. In KEYS pads play pitches. With SOUND or TEMPO held they type numbers.

Official guide: Play and record

Enter

Confirm / song list

Confirms settings, such as typed system codes. Hold MAIN and press ENTER to open the song list. SHIFT + ENTER goes back a page in system settings.
