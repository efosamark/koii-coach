The controls

Not to scale. Positions match the labelled drawing in the official guide’s Hardware overview.

The small words above the pads name what each pad controls while you hold FADER.

Modes

  • SOUND chooses and shapes a pad’s sound.
  • MAIN plays patterns, scenes and songs.
  • TEMPO sets the speed and metronome.
  • TIMING sets the grid and swing.
  • SAMPLE records sound, not notes.
X, Y and − / + do a different job in each mode.

Official guide: Modes

Every control

MAIN

Home

Press it to come back from any mode. Here you write and play patterns. Hold MAIN and press − / + to change scene. SHIFT + MAIN commits: it saves the scene and moves you into a copy. Hold MAIN and press ENTER for the song list. Hold MAIN and a pad from 1–9 to change project, and check the display.

Official guide: Modes

SOUND

Choose a sound

Select the group and tap the pad first. − / + browses samples, and SHIFT + − / + jumps by ten. X sets the volume, Y the pitch. SHIFT + SOUND opens sound edit.

Official guide: Modes

TEMPO

Speed and metronome

X sets the BPM, Y the metronome volume. Hold TEMPO and type a number for an exact BPM. MAIN + TEMPO sets the time signature. SHIFT + TEMPO loops part of the pattern live.

Official guide: Modes

TIMING

Grid and groove

X sets the grid, usually 16 steps per bar. Y adds swing. − snaps your recording to the grid. + keeps it as played. SHIFT + TIMING snaps notes already recorded. Hold TIMING and a pad for note repeat.

Official guide: Functions

SAMPLE

Record sound

− / + chooses the source: mic, line in, usb or rsp (your own beat). X sets the input level, Y the threshold. Hold a pad to record and let go to stop. SHIFT + SAMPLE opens chop.

Official guide: Functions

KEYS

Pitch keyboard

Select a pad with a sound, then press KEYS: the pads play it at 12 pitches. Hold KEYS and press − / + for the octave, turn X for the key, Y for the scale. Hold TIMING and several pads for an arpeggio. It needs a sound set to oneshot or legato (Chapter 4).

Official guide: Play and record

FX

Effects

All groups share one effect, chosen with − / +. The fader sets how much of the group goes in. Hold FX and press pads for punch-in effects, or a group button to solo it. SHIFT + FX opens the compressor and sidechain.

Official guide: Effects

Fader

Slider

Group volume, or effect amount in FX. Hold FADER and a pad to control what’s printed above it. Record a move by holding RECORD.

Official guide: Play and record

Knob X

By mode

SOUND: volume. TEMPO: BPM. TIMING: grid. SAMPLE: input level. MAIN: input gain. KEYS held: key. Trim page: start. FX: the effect’s length, cutoff or drive. SHIFT + FX: compressor drive. Sidechain: dip length. Hold SHIFT while turning for finer steps.

Official guide: Modes · Effects

Knob Y

By mode

SOUND: pitch. TEMPO: metronome. TIMING: swing. SAMPLE: threshold. Trim page: length. MAIN: how much input goes to the effect. KEYS held: scale. FX: the effect’s colour, feedback or resonance. SHIFT + FX: compressor speed. Sidechain: dip shape.

Official guide: Modes · Effects

Minus

− / +

SOUND: picks the sample. Stopped MAIN: one step, or a bar with SHIFT. Group held: changes its pattern. MAIN held: changes scene. RECORD held: removes a bar.

Official guide: Modes

Plus

− / +

Like −, the other way. RECORD held: adds a bar. Song list: moves between positions. SAMPLE: chooses the source.

Official guide: Modes

SHIFT

Second functions

SHIFT + A: next empty pattern. SHIFT + B: undo. SHIFT + C: copy. SHIFT + D: paste. SHIFT + MAIN: commit. SHIFT + SOUND: sound edit. SHIFT + SAMPLE: chop. SHIFT + TIMING: timing correct. SHIFT + FX: compressor and sidechain. SHIFT + ERASE: system settings. Never hold SHIFT + ERASE while switching on: that formats the drive.

Official guide: Modes

ERASE

Use with care

Stopped in MAIN, a quick ERASE + pad removes the note on this step. Hold both until TRK blinks to clear all that pad’s notes in the pattern. ERASE + MAIN held for two seconds erases the scene. ERASE + SOUND deletes the selected sample for good. SHIFT + ERASE held while switching on formats the drive and removes the factory sounds for good.

Official guide: Play and record

RECORD

Record notes

Tap, then PLAY: record live after a count-in. Hold with a pad: write a note. Hold with +: add a bar. Tap while recording: stop recording, keep playing. Hold while moving the fader: record the move.

Official guide: Play and record

PLAY

Start / stop

Starts and stops playback. Stop before placing single notes. In the song list, SHIFT + PLAY plays from the start.

Official guide: Play and record

Group A

Group A

12 pads with their own patterns. Chapter 1 uses A1 kick, A2 snare and A3 hat. In MAIN, SHIFT + A finds the next empty pattern. In the song list, it adds a position.

Official guide: Modes

Group B

Group B

12 more pads. Chapter 1 uses B1 for bass. SHIFT + B is undo while the umbrella is lit.

Official guide: Play and record

Group C

Group C

Chapter 2 puts the melody on C1. SHIFT + C copies: press once for the bar, twice for the whole pattern.

Official guide: Play and record

Group D

Group D

A good home for your own samples. SHIFT + D pastes what you copied.

Official guide: Get started

Number pads

Performance pads

Tap to hear it. Hold RECORD and tap to write a note. A lit pad has a note on this step. In SAMPLE, hold to record. In KEYS, they play pitches. With SOUND or TEMPO held, they type numbers.

Official guide: Play and record

Enter

Confirm / song list

Confirms a typed system code. Hold MAIN and press ENTER for the song list. In system settings, SHIFT + ENTER goes back a page.
